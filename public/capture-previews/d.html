Planning your visit

A few questions before you come in.

How do I arrange a consultation?+
Contact the office with a brief description of the topic and your preferred times. The appointment details and fee are confirmed before your visit.
What should I bring?+
Bring relevant documents, correspondence and a list of important dates. Keep original records with you unless another arrangement is specifically agreed.
Can I ask about fees first?+
Yes. Ask about the consultation fee and the scope and cost of any further work before proceeding.
Does requesting an appointment start legal work?+
An appointment request is an enquiry. The scope and terms of any work are discussed separately with the office.
