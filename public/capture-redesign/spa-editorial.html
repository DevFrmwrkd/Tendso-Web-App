Good to know.

Should I book in advance?+

Please get in touch before coming by so the sample spa can confirm the available time, room and session.

Can I ask about the session before booking?+

Yes. Ask about the duration, approach, price and room options before deciding on your visit.

Tell the team about your preferences and anything that may affect the suitability of a session. Ask any questions you have before arranging the visit.
