Behind Likha Wood Studio

The details make the difference.

Likha Wood Studio↗

The pieces we use every day become part of the room around them. We like a practical shape, a comfortable proportion and a finish that lets the grain have its say.

Start with a sketch, a few measurements or a photo of the space. We’ll discuss the material, construction and finish, then agree on the dimensions, scope and quote before making begins.

A few useful answers

Before you
come by.

Something else on your mind? Get in touch and we’ll help with your question.

Can I ask for a custom size?+

Share the dimensions, intended use and any references. The workshop can discuss what it can make and prepare a quote for the agreed design.

How long will a piece take?+

The schedule depends on the design, material and current work. Confirm the estimated timing and any changes directly with the workshop.

Can I visit before deciding?+

Get in touch to arrange a workshop visit. Bring your measurements or references so the conversation can focus on the piece you have in mind.
