Before you come by

Good to know.

How do I arrange a consultation?+

Contact the office with your availability and the general topic you want to discuss. Confirm the appointment details and consultation fee before visiting.

What should I bring?+

Bring complete copies of relevant documents, related correspondence and a simple timeline of important dates. Ask the office if anything else is needed.

Does an appointment request start legal work?+

An inquiry or appointment request is a first contact. Any professional engagement, proposed work and fees would need to be discussed and agreed with the real office.
