Editor's note

I photograph the in-between moments, the nervous laugh before the aisle and the quiet minute after the vows, in the soft light that Tagaytay is known for.

Liwanag means light. It is also the name my grandmother gave our family's little photo shop in Silang, where I learned to load film before I learned to ride a bike.

Today it is just me and one assistant. I take a limited number of weddings each year, so I can give every couple the same calm, unhurried day.
