01 · The shop

Two bays, one crane,
no middlemen

Bakal Fabrication Works is a steel fabrication and welding shop on Governor's Drive in Dasmariñas. If it is made of metal and it has to fit something you already own, this is the kind of shop you bring the measurement to.

It started as a one-bay stall repairing tricycle frames. It is now two bays under a five-tonne gantry, a CNC plasma table and eight full-time fabricators, most of whom trained here.

We do not subcontract. The people who quote your job cut it, weld it, finish it and bolt it in. If something is off on site, the person who made it is the one standing there with a grinder.

10 · Before you message

Asked at the gate

Do I need a proper drawing?
No. A phone sketch with rough dimensions is enough to start. We measure on site and produce the shop drawing you approve.
How do you quote?
In writing, after measuring. Material, labour, finish and installation are itemised so you can see what changes if you change the spec.
Do you install, or just fabricate?
Both. Most jobs are installed by the same crew that built them. Fabricate-only is fine if you have your own installers.
Can I supply my own steel?
Yes, though we will check it before cutting. We cannot guarantee the finish on material we did not source.
What about rust?
Mild steel is primed and either epoxy-painted or powder coated. Anything near the coast or a kitchen, we recommend stainless and will say so.
Do you do small repairs?
Yes. Bring it to the yard, or send a photo first and we will tell you if it is worth fixing.
