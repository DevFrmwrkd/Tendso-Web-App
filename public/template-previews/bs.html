Our story

It started with one bucket of kalachuchi.

Bea sold her first bouquets from a folding table on Maginhawa Street. Ten years on, the table is a studio — and the neighbours still come by for Friday flowers.

We buy at Dangwa before sunrise and from growers in Benguet twice a week, so nothing sits in a chiller for days. Every bouquet is made by hand the morning it leaves.

Weddings are styled by the same small team, from the first mock-up at the studio to the last centrepiece at the reception.

Questions

Before you order

Anything else, just message us — we usually reply within the hour.

What's the same-day cut-off?+

Order by 2 PM for delivery the same day within Quezon City. Later orders go out first thing the next morning.

How much is delivery?+

It depends on the distance. We quote the rider fee with your order, before you pay.

Can you use a specific flower?+

Usually, yes. Tell us early — peonies, tulips and some imported blooms need two to three days to source.

How early should we book a wedding?+

Three to six months is ideal, especially for December and summer dates. Small civil ceremonies can be done in a few weeks.

Do you deliver to chapels and memorial parks?+

Yes. Sympathy sprays and wreaths go straight to the chapel, with your card placed on the stand.
