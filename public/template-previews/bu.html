The process

From first call to the last petal

Weddings usually take three to four meetings over a few months.

  1. I

    Enquiry

    Send your date, venue and guest count. We confirm availability within a day.

  2. II

    Consultation

    At the atelier, with mood boards, swatches and flowers in hand.

  3. III

    Proposal & mock-up

    A written design proposal, then a full table mock-up once you approve.

  4. IV

    The day

    We install, stay through the ceremony, and strike after the last dance.

Questions

Good to ask early

How far ahead do you book weddings?+

Six to twelve months for peak dates. We take a limited number of weddings each weekend.

Is there a minimum for events?+

We share our minimums at the first call, as they depend on the date and venue.

Do you travel outside Metro Manila?+

Yes — Tagaytay, Batangas and Pampanga regularly, and further by arrangement.

Can I order a bouquet without an appointment?+

Of course. Message us on Viber or WhatsApp by 1 PM for same-day delivery.
