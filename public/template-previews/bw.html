A typical kitchen is three weeks in the shop and one day on site.

    Fabrication

  4. 04

    Installation

    Fitted, levelled and cleaned up, usually in a single day.

07 — FAQ

Common questions

Is the site measure really free?[+]

Yes, within our Metro Manila coverage. Outside it, the travel fee is credited back if you go ahead.

Can you work with my interior designer?[+]

Often. We build from their drawings and send back shop drawings for approval before cutting.

What finishes can I choose?[+]

High-pressure laminate, 2K lacquer, wood veneer or solid wood. We bring samples to your site measure.

Do you remove old cabinets?[+]

Yes. Demolition and haul-away can be added to the quote.
