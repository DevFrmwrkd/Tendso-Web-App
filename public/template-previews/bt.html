About us

Two sisters, one very red shopfront.

Mica and Jo opened Gumamela on Salinas Drive in 2018 with a single cooler and a scooter. The scooter is now three delivery vans.

We like colour, and we're not shy about it. If you want something quieter, say so — we'll still make it feel alive.

Our flowers come from growers in Dalaguete and Busay, plus a weekly shipment from Manila for the blooms Cebu can't grow.

Questions, answered

What's the same-day cut-off?+

3 PM for delivery anywhere in Metro Cebu. After that, we deliver the next morning.

Can I send flowers to someone in a hospital?+

Yes. Some wards don't allow fresh flowers, so we'll check with the hospital first and suggest an alternative if needed.

Do you style weddings outside Cebu City?+

We regularly style weddings in Mactan, Moalboal and Bohol. Travel and set-up are quoted with your proposal.

How far ahead should I book an event?+

Three months for weddings, four to six weeks for debuts and company events.
