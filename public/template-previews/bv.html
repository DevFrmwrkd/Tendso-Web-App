The workshop

Three generations in the same workshop in Guagua.

Lolo Ben started making church pews and aparadors in 1987. His grandchildren now run the shop with eleven carpenters, most trained right here.

We still cut joints by hand, dry our own lumber and finish every piece with oil or lacquer in the same room it was built.

Our wood comes from permitted suppliers and reclaimed houses in Central Luzon, and we'll show you the paperwork if you ask.

Questions

Before you order

Anything else, send us a message on Viber or WhatsApp.

Can you copy a design from a photo?+

Yes. Send us the photo and the size you need, and we'll draw it up and suggest changes that suit solid wood.

Which woods do you work with?+

Mostly narra, acacia, mahogany and reclaimed yakal. Availability changes, so we confirm when we quote.

How long does a custom piece take?+

Four to eight weeks after you approve the drawing, depending on size and finish.

Can I visit the workshop?+

Yes, the showroom is next to the workshop. Message us first so someone can walk you through.
