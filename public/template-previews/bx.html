About the studio

Paolo and Grace Uy started Acacia Studio in 2004 making furniture for export. Today we make it for Cebu first — for homes, for the resorts on Mactan, and for anyone who wants a piece that will outlast the house.

Our weavers are from Danao and Compostela, and most of our carpenters have been with us for over ten years.

We use plantation acacia and mahogany, and reclaimed hardwood where we can find it.

Questions

Can I change the size of a catalogue piece?+

Yes. Most pieces can be resized, and we'll confirm any change to lead time when we quote.

Do you take bulk orders for hotels and cafés?+

Yes. We make a prototype first, then produce the full run once it's approved.

Is rattan OK outdoors?+

Natural rattan is best under a roof. For open-air spaces we use synthetic rattan on treated frames.

How do I pay?+

A 50% deposit to start, and the balance before delivery. Bank transfer, GCash or card at the showroom.
