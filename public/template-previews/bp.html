Good to know

Questions we get asked

The four we answer most on the phone.

Do you take walk-ins

Two chairs are held back every day. Before noon is the best chance.

How long is a cut

Forty-five minutes for a cut, seventy for a cut and beard.

Do you cut children

Yes, from about six, when sitting still is realistic.

Which card do you take

All of them, and cash. There is no card minimum.
