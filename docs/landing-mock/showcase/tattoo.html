Studio notes

Small tattoos, drawn by hand and done in one sitting. We keep the studio bright, the music low and the lines delicate.

How it works

Four easy steps
1

Send a reference

Message us a photo, a doodle or just a feeling, plus where on the body you want it.

2

We draw it

Mika sketches your design and sends it within three days. Changes are free.

3

Stencil and approve

On the day, we place the stencil on your skin. Move it until it feels right.

4

Tattoo and aftercare

A clean, calm session, then a printed aftercare card and a check-in message after a week.

Meet the artist
