When to
come

Siargao surfs all year. Here is what the water is doing.

Aug – Nov
Peak swell

Powerful, hollow waves at Cloud 9 for experienced surfers. Beginners head to the softer breaks at high tide.

Dec – Mar
Smaller and friendly

Cooler mornings, clean waves and fewer people in the water. Our favourite time for first lessons.

Apr – Jul
Hot and glassy

Warm, flat-ish mornings, ideal for longboards and for learning to read the waves.
