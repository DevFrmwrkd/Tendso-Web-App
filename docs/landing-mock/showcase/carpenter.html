The maker

Mang Andoy, third-generation carver

“In Paete, we say the wood tells you what it wants to be. I just listen, then measure twice.”

Andres Cagayat learned carving from his lolo for the town’s saints and fiestas. Now he and his two sons make furniture for families all over Luzon.
