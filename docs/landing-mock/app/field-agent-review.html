The final polish pass.

Use the field-agent test account. Check these in the app, then record the outcome beside each panel.

  1. Images and map sheetOpen a shop with no photo, a broken photo, and each shop type. Drag the sheet up and down; cards keep their height and illustrations fit their boxes.
  2. Small screens, larger text, keyboardReach every field and action on capture, profile correction and cashout. Check long names, emails, distances and amounts.
  3. Permissions and interruptionsTry camera, microphone and location denial. Pause or background an interview and recover the saved take.
  4. Offline captureQueue a capture, reconnect with the app open, and check that partial uploads and errors can recover without duplicate submissions.
  5. Unfinished capturesClose the app before sending, reopen and tap Submit. Check the same shop and saved step return. Change its business type and verify all photos still open; try an uploaded photo with a missing local copy and replace only an unrecoverable unsent photo.
  6. Navigation and notificationsLesson Back returns to training. Fix and resend preserves the quiz pass. Opening the bell leaves untouched notifications unread.
  7. MoneyCompare credited earnings, available balance and payout statuses. Review sample cashout screens here; real transfers are a separate test.
