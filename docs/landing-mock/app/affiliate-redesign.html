What changes

Less guessing.
More useful detail.

Four consistent tabsHome, Agents, Money and Me. Sales opens from Home or Me; pushed screens use Back.

Clear agent periods“9 months left” sits beside a dark-on-grey track. An ended period says so directly. No “Simulated progress” labels in the product design.

Money that adds up₱4,250 from link sales + ₱700 from agents = ₱4,950 earned this month. ₱7,200 is the separate available balance.

One app, real account dataThe mobile implementation uses the signed-in creator’s income, agents, sales and payout history. This board remains an isolated sample.

One fixed creator-code offerEvery code gives 30% off: the shop pays ₱3,499, saves ₱1,500 and the creator’s share is ₱1,750. Your offer is read-only; historical sales keep their agreed amounts.

Save without covering your fieldsThe page editor keeps its status and Save page button below its scroll area. The preview and sharing use saved photos, messages and social links.

Read one message at a timeOpen a notification to read its full text. Untouched messages remain unread unless you choose “Mark all read”.
