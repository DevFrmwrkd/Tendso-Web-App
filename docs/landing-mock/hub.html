Original design notes

20–21 Oct 2026 · archive
View the original handoff questions

Historical context from the first handoff. These questions are not a current product-status checklist.

  1. Payment rule. Turnaround is now "same day, about 30 minutes". "Pay only when it's live" was wrong; copy says "See it first. Pay once to publish." Confirm that's right.
  2. Prices in the "Pay once" comparison. Placeholders: web agency ₱10,000 a month, Wix or Webflow ₱1,500 a month, shown over one year against Tendso ₱4,999 once.
  3. App Store and Google Play links. QR codes are drawn patterns until then.
  4. "Field agent" or "Tendso creator"? Production says creator; the brief says field agent. Pages use both once, then "agent".
  5. Real photos of the team and agents. People photos are generated placeholders, Filipino first.
  6. Business-owner tab bar. Five tabs (Home, Site, Help, Inbox, Account) or four.
  7. Old pricing copy inside the app. help-faq, terms of service and the price slider still mention the removed unlock rule.
