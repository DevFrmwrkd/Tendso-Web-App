How a visit works

One visit.
About 30 minutes.

The app tells you what to do, one step at a time.

  2. The owner talks on camera. The app shows one question at a time.

  3. The app shows you each shot. Hold still, tap.

  4. The site is ready the same day, about 30 minutes later. The owner sees the finished site first, then pays once to publish.
