Will you have to drill my lock?

Rarely. The great majority of lockouts open non-destructively — picking, bumping or manipulating — which leaves your existing lock working and saves you the cost of a replacement. Sometimes a high-security or damaged lock has to be drilled, but we'll always tell you before we do it and why, never after.

How much is a call-out?

We agree the full price on the phone before we set off, based on what you describe — not a teaser "call-out fee" that balloons on your doorstep. Barring something genuinely unexpected (which we'd explain first), the number we quote is the number you pay. No per-minute meter, ever.

How fast can you get to me in an emergency?

For lockouts across Norwich and the NR postcodes, usually within the hour and often much faster. When you call we'll give you a realistic time, not a fantasy one — and if someone closer could genuinely help you sooner, we'll say so.

Can you make sure old keys don't work?

Yes — changing or rekeying the lock means any previously cut key stops working, so only the people you want holding a key can get in. It's one of our most common jobs and usually done same day. If you just want reassurance, we'll advise what's worth doing.

Will an upgrade actually satisfy my insurance?

It will if it's specified to your policy — which is why we read the exact wording (often "BS3621" or an anti-snap requirement) before fitting, rather than guessing. Fitting the wrong thing can void a claim, so we get it right and give you the paperwork to prove it.

My uPVC door only locks if I lift the handle just right. Fixable?

Almost always, yes — it's a worn multipoint mechanism or a door that's dropped out of alignment, and both are repairable without replacing the whole door. Left alone it eventually fails shut or open, so it's worth sorting before it strands you.
