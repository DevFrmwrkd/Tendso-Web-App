ROUND 01 / THE GYMA FIGHTER'S GYM FOR EVERYONE

A fighter's gym that welcomes everyone.

Southpaw was set up by two former amateurs who were tired of two kinds of place: the intimidating fight gym that ignores anyone who isn't competing, and the shiny "boxing-inspired" studio where nobody learns to actually box. This is the third thing — real coaching, real technique, open to the nervous first-timer and the club fighter alike.

Everyone starts on the pads and the bags, learning stance, guard and the four punches properly before anything else. You will not be thrown into a ring in week one. You may never spar at all, and plenty of our members never do — the skill and the fitness are the point, and they arrive whether or not you ever take a shot.

There are no mirror walls to pose in front of and no thumping playlist to hide behind. Just the sound of skipping ropes, the bags, the bell on the round timer, and a coach watching your feet. It's the calmest loud place in Leeds.

ROUND 04 / THE FIRST SIX WEEKSDOOR → STANCE → BAG → PATH
  1. Step 01

    Book the course

    Six weeks, one group, starting monthly. No kit but shorts and a water bottle — we lend gloves and wraps until you want your own.

  2. Step 02

    Learn the four punches

    Stance, guard, jab, cross, hook, uppercut — done properly and slowly before speed. Your feet get more attention than your fists at first.

  3. Step 03

    Put it on the bag

    Rounds on the bags and pads, building the fitness and the timing. This is where it clicks and where the week's stress goes to die.

  4. Step 04

    Choose your path

    Stay for fitness, push into technical classes, or — only if you want — try supervised sparring. All three are full memberships, no hierarchy.

Anything else — ring 0113 244 8890. Mid-session it's loud; leave a message and we'll ring back between classes.

I've genuinely never boxed. Am I going to embarrass myself?

No — the beginners' course is built entirely for you, and everyone in the room started in exactly the same spot. We teach stance and the punches from zero, slowly, with no assumed knowledge. The only people who look out of place here are the ones pretending they already know it all.

Will I have to spar or get hit?

Never unless you choose to. Sparring is entirely optional at Southpaw, forever — lots of members train for years and never do it, and they get the full benefit of the fitness and the skill. If you do decide to try, it's supervised, size-matched, protected, and never a surprise.

Is it just for young, fit people?

Not at all. Our members run from teenagers to people in their sixties, every fitness level, all shapes. Boxing scales to whoever's doing it — the coach adjusts the work to you, not the other way round. Plenty of people arrive to get fit, not arrive already fit.

What do I need to bring?

Shorts or joggers, a t-shirt, trainers and a water bottle for the first few weeks — we lend gloves and hand wraps until you're sure you're sticking around. When you are, we'll help you buy the right gloves rather than the flashiest ones.

Do you do women-only classes?

Yes — dedicated women-only sessions with the same technique-first coaching, plus mixed classes that are welcoming to everyone. Many members move between the two. If a women-only start is what gets you through the door, it's here and it's properly coached.

Can my kids train here?

We run coached youth classes with the same discipline-and-technique approach — no full-contact for juniors, plenty of skill, fitness and focus. It's brilliant for confidence. Come and watch a session before you sign them up; parents usually end up joining too.
