PASS 01 / THE BENCHFORE STREET · STONES SOAKED DAILY

One bench. Eleven stones. Zero machines.

Burr & Steel started as a market stall with three stones and a queue that wouldn't end. The shop on Fore Street came two years later; the philosophy hasn't moved: machines heat steel and eat blades, so everything here is done by hand, on stones, the slow correct way.

The bench runs eleven waterstones from #400 for repairs to #8000 for the edges that make chefs go quiet. Every knife is assessed first — steel, grind, damage, how you actually use it — because a workhorse Victorinox and a single-bevel yanagiba want completely different edges.

Half our work arrives by post now: knives rolled in cardboard, taped into tubes, once memorably inside a pool noodle. All fine. The pool noodle was, honestly, excellent.

PASS 03 / THE ANGLESHELD, NOT GUESSED

Held angles.

0°

Stones, never wheels.

Powered wheels overheat steel and ruin tempers — the metallurgical kind and eventually yours. Hand sharpening keeps the steel's hardness and your knife's lifespan intact.

12°

The edge matches the knife.

A camping knife gets a tough 25°; a sushi knife gets a screaming 12°. One-angle-fits-all is how supermarkets sharpen. We're not a supermarket.

✓

Tested, then proven.

Every knife leaves through a sheet of paper — sliced, not torn — and the sheet goes back in the wrap with your knife. Receipts, literally.

PASS 07 / ASKED AT THE COUNTERKNIFE WRAPPED, HOPEFULLY

Asked at the counter.

Anything else — ring 01392 424 771. Wet hands mean the machine answers first; leave a message, we're mid-pass.

How much does sharpening cost?

Priced per blade by length and condition, posted on the wall and the website. Repairs and regrinds get quoted after assessment — we text before any stone touches steel. No surprise line items, ever.

How long will you keep my knives?

Drop-offs: usually forty-eight hours, same-day if you're desperate and ask nicely before ten. Post: back within the week, tracked. Trade rolls: overnight on the rota.

How do I post knives safely (and legally)?

Wrap each blade in cardboard, tape it closed, tube or box, tracked service, over-18 signature. Full guide on the site. UK law is fine with sharpening post — it cares about who signs, so someone over eighteen must.

How often should knives be sharpened?

Home kitchens: once or twice a year, with honing (steeling) in between — they're different things and we'll show you the difference free at the counter. Pro kitchens: on the rota, every few weeks.

Can you sharpen serrated / ceramic / that weird one?

Serrated yes, tooth by tooth. Ceramic mostly no — it wants diamond wheels we don't run, and we'll say so rather than experiment on your knife. The weird one: bring it in, the bench loves a puzzle.

Will sharpening damage my expensive Japanese knife?

Not here. Waterstones are exactly what those knives are designed for — it's the pull-through gadgets and powered wheels that kill them. Single-bevel work is a speciality of the bench, not an adventure.
