ISSUE 01 / THE SHOPORIGIN STORY

A comic shop is a reading rec with a till.

We opened in 2013 with one belief that hasn't shifted: the job isn't selling comics, it's matching people to the one book that turns them into a reader. That's as true for the kid clutching pocket money as for the collector chasing a key first appearance. Both get the same attention and neither gets talked down to.

Wednesday is the heartbeat. New issues hit the wall the day they release, the pull-lists are bagged and waiting behind the counter, and the shop fills with people arguing happily about last week's cliffhanger. Miss a Wednesday and your titles are safe — that's the whole point of a pull-list.

Behind the new wall are the bins: decades of back issues, bagged and boarded, priced fairly and graded honestly. We'll never oversell a book's condition to make a sale — a reputation for straight grading is worth more than any single key issue.

ISSUE 07 / ASKED AT THE COUNTERNO SUCH THING AS A DUMB ONE

Asked at the counter.

Anything else — ring 0115 924 7781. Wednesdays are hectic, so we may call you back once the rush clears.

What actually is a pull-list?

It's a standing order for your comics. You tell us which ongoing series you follow, and every Wednesday we pull those issues off the new shipment, bag and board them, and hold them in your named folder behind the counter. You collect whenever suits — weekly, monthly, whenever you're passing. It's free to set up, there's no minimum, and you can add, drop or pause titles any time. It means you never miss an issue and never lose out when a hot book sells through in an hour.

I've never read comics — where do I start?

With a conversation and zero judgement. Tell us what films, shows or books you love and roughly how much time you want to give it, and we'll put the right complete graphic novel or first volume in your hands. Starting with a self-contained book beats jumping into issue #347 of anything. That's the single most common visit we get, and it's our favourite.

Do you buy old comics?

Often, yes — bring them in and we'll go through them with you. We grade honestly in front of you, explain what drives the value (or doesn't), and make a fair offer on anything we can sell. We'll also tell you plainly when something's worth keeping rather than selling. No pressure, no lowballing; a straight deal is the only kind worth doing.

How does grading work here?

We grade to recognised condition standards and we call it straight — a Very Fine is a Very Fine, not a wishful Near Mint. Keys go in the cabinet with the grade on the sleeve; reading copies go in the bins, priced accordingly. If you're considering professional CGC slabbing we'll pre-screen the book and give you an honest view on whether it's worth the fee.

Is the shop kid-friendly?

Very. There's a dedicated all-ages section at kid height, a kids' comic club, and staff who genuinely enjoy matching young readers to the right series. Free Comic Book Day in May is built around families. Comics turned a lot of us into readers as children and we take passing that on seriously.

What happens on Free Comic Book Day?

The first Saturday in May, we hand out free comics, run the shop like a small festival, and usually pull in creators for signings and sketches. It's busy, it's brilliant, and it's the best day of our year. Follow us or ask in-store closer to the date for the line-up and timings.
