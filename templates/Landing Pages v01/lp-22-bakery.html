01 / THE BAKERYONE OVEN, ONE STARTER

One oven. One starter. No shortcuts.

Ferment & Flour started as a market stall with thirty loaves and a folding table. The oven came from a pizzeria that closed in Harrogate; the starter, Doris, came from a baker in Copenhagen and is old enough to have opinions.

Everything runs on an eighteen-hour ferment. Flour is stoneground English wheat from two mills we can name, and the salt list ends there — because that's the whole ingredient list.

We're four bakers working through the night and two on the counter from six. If the shelf is empty it isn't a supply problem; bread this slow can't be rushed into existing twice in one day.

07 / ASKED AT THE COUNTERSEVEN ANSWERS

Asked over the counter.

Anything else — ring 0113 262 8845 after six, when the ovens quieten down.

Can I reserve a loaf?

Yes — message us before 9am and we'll put your name on a bag. Reserved loaves are held until noon, then they go back on the shelf.

Why do you sell out?

The oven holds sixty loaves per bake and the ferment takes eighteen hours, so today's supply was decided yesterday afternoon. We'd rather sell out than bake fast bread.

Is everything sourdough?

The breads, yes. The pastries use a laminated dough with a sourdough sponge — butter does the rest of the work.

Do you do gluten-free?

Honestly, no — everything shares one oven and one bench, so we can't promise a safe loaf and won't pretend otherwise. The Buckwheat Bakehouse in Meanwood does it properly.

What happens to unsold bread?

Sliced and delivered to the community fridge on Harrogate Road the same evening. Nothing is binned; day-old bread makes the best toast anyway.

Can my café stock your bread?

If you're inside the ring road, probably. Wholesale is capped by oven space, so there's sometimes a short waiting list — email and we'll be straight with you.

Why 'Doris'?

The starter arrived from Copenhagen in a jam jar labelled D.O.R. — we never found out what it stood for. She's fourteen and fed twice daily, which is more than most of the staff.
