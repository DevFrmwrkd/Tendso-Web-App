BATCH 01 / THE ROASTERYTHE ROAST DATE IS THE POINT

The roast date is the whole point.

Coffee is fruit, and like fruit it has a season on the shelf. It hits its best a few days after roasting and slowly fades for weeks after. Yet most coffee is roasted in vast batches, warehoused, and sold with a use-by date a year out and no roast date at all — because the roast date would give the game away.

Kiln does the opposite. We roast small batches on a fifteen-kilo cast-iron drum in Kelham Island, stamp the roast date on every bag, and roast to order so what leaves us is days old, not months. You can taste the difference the first morning, and you never go back.

We buy green coffee from importers who name the farm and pay the grower properly, roast each origin to bring out what it actually is rather than to a one-size "house" char, and write honest tasting notes — if it tastes of digestive biscuits, that's what the bag says, not "notes of bergamot and stone fruit".

Why does a roast date matter more than a use-by date?

Because coffee is at its best days after roasting and fades over the following weeks — the use-by date (often a year out) tells you when it's technically safe, not when it's good. A roast date lets you drink coffee in its window. If a bag hides its roast date, ask yourself why.

Whole bean or ground — which should I buy?

Whole bean if you possibly can, and grind just before brewing — ground coffee goes stale in days, whole beans last weeks. If you don't have a grinder, we'll grind to your exact brewer (we ask which one), but a modest home grinder is the single best upgrade to your coffee.

How fresh will my coffee be when it arrives?

We roast to order and dispatch fast, so beans typically reach you within days of roasting — near their peak, with the roast date stamped so you can see. Let it rest a day or two after roasting for the best results; the bag tells you the roast date so you can time it.

I usually drink dark, strong coffee. Do you do that?

We do roasts on the darker end, yes — but roasted to bring out sweetness and body, not burnt to hide a flat bean. Give our medium-dark a fair go before you decide; a lot of "I only like it strong" turns out to be "I only like it fresh". We'll happily steer you.

Can my café buy from you?

Yes — fresh-roast wholesale with your beans dialled in on your machine, barista training, and kit support. Sheffield deliveries by bike, further afield by courier. We keep the wholesale list small so every account gets proper attention, so do get in touch early.

Do you ship nationwide?

All over the UK, roasted to order for each dispatch, with the subscription posting on your chosen schedule. Local to Sheffield you can collect from the roastery or catch us at the market — and collection means you can grab beans the same day they're bagged.
