TURN 01 / THE CAFÉTHE WALL IS THE MENU

The wall is the menu.

Meeple & Mug started because its founders kept hosting game nights that outgrew their flat. The idea was simple: a proper café — real coffee, real food — wrapped around a library big enough that you'd never run out, and staff who could teach any of it so nobody's stuck reading a rulebook while the tea goes cold.

The wall holds around eight hundred games, sorted by how long they take and how much brain they need, with a little card on each shelf telling you players, time and difficulty. Lost? Tell the Games Guru what your group's like and how long you've got, and you'll have the right game — taught — inside ten minutes.

It's five pounds a person for the table, all day, however many games you get through. Come for an hour, come for the afternoon, come with your nan or your work team or a first date you're testing under pressure. The kettle and the shelves don't mind either way.

TURN 07 / ASKED AT THE COUNTERUSUALLY MID-SETUP

Asked at the counter.

Anything else — ring 0115 924 6633. If we're mid-teach, leave a message and we'll ring back between games.

I don't know any board games. Is that a problem?

It's the whole reason we exist. Tell the Games Guru you're a total beginner and they'll start you on something brilliant and simple, taught from scratch in a few minutes. Nobody here expects you to know anything — most of our regulars started exactly the same way.

How much does it cost to play?

Five pounds per person for the table, and that's it — play as many games as you like, stay as long as you like, all afternoon if you fancy. Food and drink are on top, but there's no per-game charge and no time limit on the table.

Do I need to book?

Weekday afternoons you can usually just walk in. Evenings and weekends get busy, so booking a table is wise — especially for groups of four or more. Learn-to-play nights and private hires should always be booked ahead.

Can you really teach us any game?

Any game on the wall, yes — that's the Games Guru's actual job. Tell us your group and your time and we'll pick something that fits, fetch it, set it up and teach the rules in about five minutes, then stay close for questions. You'll never be left with just a rulebook.

Is it good for kids / families / older relatives?

Brilliant for all of them. The library is carded by age and complexity, so there's something for a six-year-old, a teenager, and a grandparent at the same table. Family afternoons are some of our busiest and happiest sessions.

Can I buy the games?

Most of the library is stocked to buy at the counter, so if a game wins your group over you can take it straight home. If we've sold out we'll order it in, and the staff are dangerously good at recommending "just one more" for your shelf.
