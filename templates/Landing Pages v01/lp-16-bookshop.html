The shop

Small shop, long memory.

The Long Sentence opened in 2016 in a crooked building in the Norwich Lanes that has been, at various points, a milliner's, a locksmith's, and — according to one customer with strong opinions — a place that sold nothing but buttons. The floors slope. The books don't mind.

Downstairs is new fiction, poetry, essays and the translation wall: four hundred titles in English from everywhere else, hand-picked, argued over, and re-shelved weekly. Upstairs is the reading room — armchairs, a kettle, and a strictly enforced rule that nobody will talk to you unless you start it.

There are three of us. Between us we've read most of the shop, and we'll say so honestly when we haven't.

Why this shop

Three promises, kept daily.

i.

Hand-picked, honestly sold.

We've read most of the shop. When we haven't, we say so. When you'll hate it, we say that too.

ii.

The fifty-page promise.

Any recommendation of ours you hate inside fifty pages comes back for a full swap. It's used about twice a month, and we like it that way.

iii.

A room to read in.

The reading room upstairs is free, quiet, and yours. Buy nothing. Stay all afternoon. The kettle is the honesty system.

Questions

Asked at the counter.

Can you order any book?

Anything in print in the UK, usually within 48 hours, at cover price. Out-of-print hunting we'll try too — no promises, no charge for looking.

How does the subscription work?

One conversation (in person or by phone) about what you love and what you've bounced off. Then a book a month, wrapped, with a letter about why we chose it. Three, six or twelve months; gift options at all lengths.

What's the fifty-page promise?

If we recommended it and you hate it within fifty pages, bring it back for a straight swap. Applies to our recommendations, not your own gambles — those you have to live with, like the rest of us.

Is the reading room really free?

Really free. No minimum, no time limit, no guilt. Tea and coffee run on an honesty tin. The only rule is quiet.

Do you buy second-hand books?

No — we're new books only, except the poetry shelf's 'orphans' box, which works on donation and rehoming rather than money.

How do Thursday events work?

Doors at seven, reading at half past. Tickets are a few pounds and come off any book that night. The translation club is the first Thursday of the month.

Is the shop accessible?

The ground floor is step-free from the Lanes. The reading room is up a 400-year-old staircase we can't alter — but we'll bring the kettle and any book down to you, gladly.

Do you do school or library orders?

Yes — educational discount, invoicing, and we'll come talk to a class about books for the price of a biscuit.
