CROP 01 / THE STALLA CALENDAR YOU CAN EAT

The shelf is a calendar you can eat.

Walk in during May and it's asparagus and Jersey Royals; come back in October and it's squash, kale and the last of the tomatoes. That's not a gap in the range — that's the whole point. We buy what's ready from farms within an hour's drive, so the stall tells you the season better than any wall chart.

Everything is traceable to a name. The eggs are from a farm up the valley, the apples from an orchard we've bought from for a decade, the leaves cut the morning they arrive. When a customer asks "where's this from", the answer is a place, a person and a postcode — never a distribution centre.

We waste almost nothing. Wonky carrots go in the box scheme, gluts become the specials board, and the very tired stuff goes to the pigs at the farm it came from. A greengrocer that throws food away has stopped paying attention.

What does "in season" actually mean here?

It means grown outdoors, in this country, ready to harvest now — not forced under heat or flown across the world to fake a season. It's why our range changes through the year and why the stall never looks the same in June as it does in December. If you want strawberries in January, we're the wrong shop, and we'll say so cheerfully.

How does the veg box work?

Choose a size, tell us what you never want and any allergies, and pick collection or local delivery. Each week we pack the best of what's ready — you can see the likely contents in advance and swap a couple of items. It's a standing order you can pause any time, not a contract.

Is everything really local?

The fruit and veg is overwhelmingly from farms and orchards within about an hour, and we name them on the boards. A few things — citrus, bananas, coffee — obviously can't be grown in Devon, and those we buy fairtrade and mark clearly. We'd rather be honest about the lemons than pretend.

Is it more expensive than the supermarket?

Some things are dearer, plenty are cheaper — especially whatever's in glut that week, which we sell for very little because we'd rather it was eaten. Buy with the season instead of against it and most people spend less, waste less and eat better. The specials board is where the bargains live.

Do you deliver, and how far?

We deliver boxes locally around Totnes and the surrounding villages a couple of days a week; further afield is collection from the shop. Trade orders we'll sort out directly — tell us where you are and we'll be straight about whether it works. Delivery days and the current area are on our Google profile.

Can you supply my café or restaurant?

Gladly — it's a good chunk of what we do. You get the same-day pick, a standing order tuned to your menu, and a direct line to whoever's on the stall so you hear about gluts and specials first. Give us a ring and we'll talk through volumes and delivery.
