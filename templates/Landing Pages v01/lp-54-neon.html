RIG 01 / THE WORKSHOPLOWER TRINITY STREET, DIGBETH

Fire, glass, gas, patience.

Tube & Transformer is a working neon shop, not a print firm with a neon-effect font. Everything starts as a straight length of glass tube and a ribbon burner at 800 degrees. The bender heats, bends, and blows by lung and by feel — there is no machine for this and there never has been.

Filled with neon, the tube burns orange-red; with argon and a drop of mercury, electric blue. Every other colour is coated glass over those two gases. We tell people this because the physics is the romance, and because it explains why a real neon sign costs what it costs and lasts what it lasts.

Half the bench is new commissions; the other half is resurrection — dead pub signs, chemist crosses, a fairground arch from 1962. If it once glowed, we can usually make it glow again.

Is this real neon or LED 'neon'?

Real glass neon — bent by hand, filled with gas, driven by a transformer. The LED stuff coiled in a channel is a different, cheaper product; some shops sell it as neon and we'd rather you knew the difference. We make both honestly, and we'll tell you which suits the job.

How much does a sign cost?

It's priced by tube length, number of colours and bends, so quotes happen from your artwork, not off a shelf. A short single-colour script is a lovely-gift sum; a big multi-colour commercial piece is a serious sum. Both last decades, which changes the maths.

How long will it take?

Most commissions three to four weeks — drawing, bending, pumping and ageing all take the time they take. Weddings: book six weeks out. Repairs depend on what's died; bring it in for a look.

Is neon safe / expensive to run?

Very safe when installed properly, and astonishingly cheap to run — a typical sign costs pennies a day. The high voltage lives inside the transformer and the wiring; you just get the glow. We install and certify for commercial use.

Can you copy my logo / handwriting?

Usually, within reason — glass can't do hairline serifs or tiny detail, so very fine logos get gently adapted and we'll show you exactly how before bending. Handwriting is a favourite; send a clear photo of the real thing.

Can you fix an old sign?

Often yes, and it's our favourite work. Dead tubes can be re-pumped, cracks re-bent, and antique transformers replaced with safe modern ones. Bring it in — even in bits — and we'll give you the honest verdict.
