01 / LA STORIAEIGHT AND A HALF

Named after the eighth-and-a-half flavour.

Otto e Mezzo — eight and a half — is named for the day we planned a permanent list of eight flavours and immediately broke the rule for a tray of greengages a customer's uncle grew in Ditchling. The half slot became the whole philosophy.

The technique is Italian: low overrun, high milk quality, served at the temperature where flavour is loudest. Paolo trained in Bologna and will tell you about it at very slight provocation.

The fruit is Sussex: strawberries from Barcombe, rhubarb from a walled garden near Lewes. Milk arrives from a named herd near Firle every second morning.

Why the queue forms.

Gelato peaks within a day of churning — so nothing here is older than that. Empty tray means come back tomorrow, and we mean it kindly.

07 / DOMANDEASKED OVER THE GLASS

Asked over the glass.

Anything else — ring 01273 691 148. If it's sunny, expect the phone to ring a while.

What's the difference between gelato and ice cream?

Less air (we churn slow), less fat (more milk than cream), and a warmer serving temperature. The result: flavour hits harder and faster. Also strong opinions, which are included free.

Do you have dairy-free flavours?

Always at least four — the fruit sorbets are water-based and the chocolate sorbet is dark enough to have a mood. Made in the same room as dairy, honestly labelled, allergen book on the counter.

Why do you sell out?

Because we churn what the morning allows and gelato is best the day it's made. Sixteen trays, once each. The alternative is serving you yesterday's — and then we'd just be an ice cream shop.

Can I buy a tub to take home?

Half-litre tubs packed from the counter, any flavours in the case. Take a freezer bag if you're walking far; Brighton seafront has opinions about frozen goods in July.

Do you do the cart for events?

Weddings, birthdays, film wraps — the cart, two flavours, cones and cups, one aproned human. Booked by the season; summer Saturdays go first.

What's in the half slot today?

Posted every morning with the day's sixteen. Ask at the counter for the story — there is always a story, usually involving someone's allotment.

Are the cones homemade?

Baked in-house every Tuesday, which is also the best-smelling day to visit. Gluten-free cones come from a dedicated bakery in Hove, sealed until you order.
