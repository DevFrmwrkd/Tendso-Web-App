The clinic

Independent, and staying that way.

Harbourside opened in 2010 and is still owned by the two vets who unlocked the door on the first morning. We are not part of a group, a chain, or a private-equity portfolio — decisions about your animal are made in this building, by people who will see the consequences at the next appointment.

The team is four vets, five nurses, and a reception desk that knows most patients by name before the door opens. We keep consultations at fifteen minutes minimum.

Continuity is the whole philosophy. You book with your vet, not whoever is free. It makes scheduling slightly harder for us and considerably better for your animal.

What we do

Care, from first jab to last kindness.

01

Consultations

Fifteen minutes minimum, with your named vet. Puppy and kitten first-visits are booked as doubles.

02

Surgery

Routine and soft-tissue surgery in-house, orthopaedics with a visiting specialist monthly.

03

Dentistry

Dental x-ray, cleaning, extractions. Every dental gets a pre-op blood panel and a follow-up check.

04

Diagnostics

Digital x-ray, ultrasound, and an in-house lab — most blood results within the hour.

05

Vaccination & preventives

Core vaccines, titre testing on request, and honest advice about which parasite products your animal actually needs.

06

End-of-life care

Home visits, quiet room appointments at the day's edges, and no clock-watching. The hardest visits get the most time.

Questions

Before you need us.

The emergency answer is first, because it matters most. Anything else, ring reception on 01326 210 447.

What do I do in an emergency?

Ring the usual number — 01326 210 447 — any hour. In opening hours you'll get reception; out of hours it diverts to the duty vet. If it's life-threatening, say so first and drive while we talk you in.

Are you taking new patients?

Yes, across Falmouth and Penryn. Registration takes five minutes and we'll request records from your previous practice ourselves.

How much is a consultation?

Fees are on our published price list at reception and sent with every registration pack. Estimates for any procedure are given in writing before you consent — no exceptions.

Do you see rabbits and small furries?

Yes — rabbits, guinea pigs, rats and most small mammals. Exotic species and birds we triage honestly: some we treat, some we refer to a specialist in Truro.

Can my nervous dog wait outside?

Please do — text from the car park and we'll fetch you straight into a room. We also run quiet clinics on Tuesday afternoons for reactive dogs and terrified cats.

Do you do home visits?

For end-of-life care, always. For routine care, where clinically sensible — ask reception. There's a call-out fee that we'll state before booking.

Do you take pet insurance?

All insurers. Direct claims with the major ones — we handle the paperwork; you pay only the excess.

What about referrals?

Orthopaedics comes to us monthly; beyond that we refer to specialists we know personally, with your full record and imaging sent ahead.
