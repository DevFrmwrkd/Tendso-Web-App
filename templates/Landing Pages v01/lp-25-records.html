A1 / THE SHOPCORNISH WORKS, TWO FLOORS

Two floors of a cutlery works, alphabetised.

Long Player opened in 2012 with three racks and a strong opinion about sleeve condition grading. It now runs to twelve thousand records over two floors — new pressings on the ground, the second-hand catalogue upstairs where the light is better for reading matrix numbers.

The stock leans where Sheffield leans: electronic, post-punk, jazz, and the endless output of this city's bedrooms. The buyer takes trade-ins four days a week with grading you can argue about but never call dishonest.

Two listening booths, rebuilt from the works' old telephone kiosks, mean you can hear anything before it goes in the bag.

C2 / FAQASKED OVER THE COUNTER

Counter talk.

Anything else — ring 0114 275 6690. If nobody answers, we're grading a collection; try again in ten.

Do you buy records?

Tuesday to Friday, cash or credit (credit pays about 20% more). For collections over a couple of hundred records we'll come to you — no obligation, and we'll tell you honestly if an auction house would serve you better.

How does grading work?

Goldmine standard, sleeve and vinyl graded separately, visible defects listed on the sticker. Play-grading on anything over £30. If your VG+ turns out to be VG, bring it back for a full refund.

Can you order something you don't stock?

If it's in print, yes — usually within a week. If it's out of print, that's what the want list is for, and we're pathologically persistent.

What's the want list service?

Free. Up to ten titles at a time, with pressing preferences if you have them. You get first refusal and a week's hold when we find it.

Do the booths cost anything?

No. Take a stack in with you. The only rule is clean hands and records back in the right sleeves — the upstairs regulars will know.

When are the in-stores?

Usually Thursdays, usually 7pm, always free. Mailing list hears first because the room only holds fifty.

Do you ship?

UK-wide, boxed like the record owes us money. International by quote. Want-list finds can be posted the day of the call if you can't get in.
