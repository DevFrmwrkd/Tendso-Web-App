BOX 01 / THE SERVICEPACKED WITH LOVE, SENT HOME

A balikbayan box is not cargo. It's love, packed by hand, sent home.

Kahon Balikbayan Cargo is a door-to-door forwarder for the boxes that hold a family together across an ocean. Every OFW knows the ritual: months of filling a box in a bedroom abroad — corned beef and chocolate, shoes for the kids, a dress for Nanay, toys, toiletries, the little luxuries that say "I'm thinking of you" — then sending it home to arrive in time for a birthday, a fiesta, Christmas. We exist to make sure it gets there, whole and on time.

We started in 2005 because too many families were being stung — boxes lost, delivery delayed for months, surprise "fees" demanded before a box would be handed over at the door. We do it differently: one clear flat rate per box quoted up front, real end-to-end tracking, and delivery genuinely to the door, whether that door is in Makati or a barangay three jeepney rides from the nearest town. Nothing more to pay when it arrives.

We forward by sea for the big regular boxes (the economical way, weeks in transit) and by air when it has to be there fast, and we cover every province and island in the country. Our people at the receiving end are Filipino, know the provinces, and treat every box like it's going to their own family — because half the time, they're delivering to families exactly like their own. Pack it with love; we'll carry it the rest of the way home.

What exactly is a door-to-door balikbayan box service?

It means we handle the box from the moment it leaves your loved one's hands overseas all the way to the moment it's placed at your family's doorstep in the Philippines — you don't touch it in between, and your family doesn't have to travel to collect it. We arrange the pick-up abroad (or you drop it at a partner point), ship it, clear it in-country, and deliver it to the actual home address, whether that's a condo in the city or a house down a barangay road far from the nearest town. 'Door-to-door' is the important phrase: some cheaper services are really 'door-to-depot,' leaving your family to arrange the final leg themselves, sometimes hours away. We don't do that. One booking, one flat rate, and the box turns up where your family actually lives.

How much does it cost, and are there fees on arrival?

We charge one clear flat rate per box for sea freight, quoted to you up front before you ship, and that is the entire cost — there is nothing more to pay when the box is delivered. This matters because the single most common horror story in this industry is a family being asked for a surprise 'release fee' or 'delivery charge' at the door before they'll be given their own box. We think that's wrong, so we don't do it: the price we quote is the price you pay, full stop, and your family owes nothing on arrival. Air freight is priced by weight rather than a flat box rate because it's a different, faster service, but the same principle holds — you know the full cost before it ships, with no charges sprung at the other end.

How long does a balikbayan box take to arrive?

By sea — the standard and most economical way — expect several weeks in transit, the exact time depending on the origin country and the destination province, plus a little more for the most remote areas. This is why we always tell families to send ahead of the occasion: if you want the box home for Christmas, a fiesta or a birthday, book the pick-up well in advance rather than cutting it fine, and we'll help you work back from the date. By air, it's days rather than weeks, the option to choose when something is urgent — medicine, documents, a last-minute gift — though it costs more and suits smaller sends. We'll give you a realistic delivery window when you book, and then you and your family can track it the whole way.

Which areas in the Philippines do you deliver to?

All of them — that's a point of pride. We deliver across the entire country: Metro Manila and the other cities, but just as importantly the provinces and the far-flung barrios, across Luzon, the Visayas and Mindanao, including the island provinces. So many OFW families come from places the big generic couriers treat as 'too remote,' and those are exactly the families a balikbayan service exists for. If your loved one's home can be reached by road, boat or the local means people actually use, we will get the box to that door. When you book, give us the complete address and any landmarks or local directions that help, and our in-country team, who know these regions, will do the rest.

What can I put in a balikbayan box, and what's not allowed?

The classics are exactly what you'd expect and entirely fine: canned goods, chocolates and non-perishable food, clothing and shoes, toys, toiletries, small household items, and the everyday little luxuries that make up a proper pasalubong. There are real rules set by customs and shipping regulations about what cannot go in — things like cash and jewellery beyond limits, certain electronics and batteries, aerosols and flammable items, perishable fresh food, and anything prohibited for import — and sending a banned item risks the whole box being delayed or held. Because these rules change and vary, the safest move is to check with us before you pack: we'll give you the current do's and don'ts, help you stay within weight and content limits, and flag anything questionable. We can also supply the boxes themselves and offer packing advice.

How do I know my box won't get lost?

Two things: real tracking, and people who treat the box as more than cargo. Every box we handle is logged and trackable from the pick-up overseas, through shipping, to delivery at the door, and you — and your family — can follow its progress rather than posting it into a void and hoping. That transparency alone prevents most of the anxiety, and it keeps us accountable at every handover. Beyond the system, our team, in-country and out, are Filipinos who understand precisely what a balikbayan box is: months of a parent's or child's wages and love, packed by hand for a family they miss. We move every box with that in mind. Nothing in shipping is ever one-hundred-percent risk-free, but between end-to-end tracking, careful handling and being genuinely reachable, we've earned the trust of families sending home for two decades.
