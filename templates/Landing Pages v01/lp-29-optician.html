01 / THE PRACTICEINDEPENDENT SINCE 1987

The examination is the point.

Fenwick & Frame has tested Cheltenham's eyes from the same practice since 1987. The founding idea hasn't changed: the eye examination is a clinical event, and it deserves more than the industry-standard twenty minutes.

Every appointment is forty minutes with the same optometrist you saw last time, wherever possible. OCT retinal scanning is included as standard. Your images are compared year on year, which is how the quiet things — glaucoma, macular changes, the odd surprise — get caught early.

The frames room is deliberately small: a few hundred frames chosen on buying trips, fitted by people who will still be here when the hinge needs tightening in three years.

07 / FAQASKED IN THE CHAIR

Better one, or better two?

Anything else — ring 01242 578 903. Urgent symptoms are triaged the same day, always.

How often should I have my eyes tested?

Every two years for most adults, annually if you're over 70, diabetic, have a family history of glaucoma, or we've told you otherwise. New symptoms — flashes, floaters, sudden changes — don't wait for the schedule; ring us the same day.

What's different about a 40-minute examination?

Time. Time to take a proper history, run the full battery unhurried, do the OCT scan, and then actually discuss what we found. The twenty-minute industry standard is a triage; this is an examination.

Is the OCT scan extra?

No — it's included in every examination, because using it selectively defeats the point. Its value is the year-on-year comparison, and that only works if everyone gets scanned every time.

Do you do NHS examinations?

Yes — NHS-funded tests for children, over-60s and eligible adults, with the same forty minutes and the same OCT scan. The funding changes; the examination doesn't.

Can I bring my own frames for new lenses?

Usually, if they're sound. We'll check the frame honestly first — reglazing a frame on its last legs wastes your lens budget, and we'll say so.

What if something urgent happens to my eyes?

Ring us. Same-day triage for flashes, floaters, painful red eyes and sudden vision changes. If it needs the hospital, we refer directly and phone ahead.

Do you sell the same frames as the chains?

Almost none. The buying trips look for independent makers and lines the multiples don't carry — not for exclusivity's sake, but because a frames room should have a point of view.
