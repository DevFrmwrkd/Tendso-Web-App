The shop

Seasonal, local, and a bit wild.

Wren & Stem opened in 2017 with one bucket of dahlias and a bicycle. The shop on Princess Victoria Street came a year later; the bicycle is still doing deliveries.

Almost everything we sell is grown within forty miles — flower farms in the Chew Valley, a walled garden near Wells, and our own cutting rows in Long Ashton. In deep winter we buy from Cornish growers before we ever look at an airport.

The style leans loose and garden-led. If you want a dozen identical red roses in February, we'll be honest — we're the wrong shop, and we'll tell you who's the right one.

What's the same-day delivery cutoff?

Order by 1pm for delivery that afternoon, anywhere in the city. The bike goes out at two and again at five.

Can I choose exact flowers?

You can steer colour and mood, and we'll always honour a no-list (no lilies, no carnations, whatever it is). Exact stems depend on the season — that's the deal with local flowers.

How far ahead do weddings book?

Nine to eighteen months for summer dates. We take twenty a year and always leave a couple of slots for short-notice registry-office jobs — ask.

Do you deliver outside Bristol?

Weddings, yes. Bouquets — city postcodes only; further out we'll recommend a florist near the recipient rather than send tired flowers in a van.

My flowers drooped in a day. Help?

Bring them back or send a photo. If it's the flowers' fault we replace them without a quiz. Usually it's the radiator's fault; we'll show you the trick.

Are your flowers pesticide-free?

Our own rows, yes. Our growers use minimal-spray methods; none are certified organic, and we'd rather be honest about that than vague.

Do you do dried flowers?

From our own late-summer drying rack, October to February. Small batches, no imported pampas.
