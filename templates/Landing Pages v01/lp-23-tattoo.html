01 / THE STUDIOTHREE ROOMS, ONE DOOR EACH

Private by design, not by attitude.

Black Letter runs three private rooms instead of an open floor. No audience, no music you didn't choose, no stranger's session bleeding into yours. It changes how people sit, and how the work comes out.

The residents cover three lanes — blackwork and ornamental, fine line and botanical, and clean western traditional. We book guests monthly for the styles we don't carry, and we'll happily tell you when someone else is the right artist for your idea.

The studio is licensed, inspected, and boringly rigorous about it. Ask for the paperwork; we like showing it off.

07 / FAQASKED BEFORE EVERY BOOKING

Before the needle.

Anything else — ring 0161 834 7702, afternoons. Mornings are for drawing.

How do I book?

Through the form — tell us what, where on the body, rough size, and any references. We reply within three working days with a consultation slot or an honest "not our lane, try these people".

How much will it cost?

You'll get a range at consultation, not a number in an Instagram DM. Deposits are fixed, come off the final session, and are the only payment we take before needle day.

Does it hurt?

Yes, variably. Ribs, feet and inner arm are the honest "quite a lot" zones. We schedule breaks, and stopping early is always allowed — the design will be split into sessions if needed.

Can you cover my old tattoo?

Maybe. Bring it in. Some covers work first pass, some need laser sessions first, and some covers would make things worse — we'll tell you which yours is, for free.

I'm 17 but I have parental consent.

It's 18 by law with photo ID, no exceptions, including with a parent present. We'd rather lose the booking than the licence.

How should I prepare?

Sleep, eat a real breakfast, skip the alcohol for 24 hours before, and wear clothes that expose the area comfortably. Bring snacks for long sits — sugar crashes are real.

What's your touch-up policy?

Free within six months if you followed aftercare. Hands, fingers and feet excepted — those blow out regardless and we'll have warned you at consultation.
