A workshop that thinks in fifty-year terms.

Mount & Mitre started in a spare room with a mitre saw and a strong opinion: most framing quietly destroys what it's meant to protect. Acid mounts burn, cheap glass fades, sticky tape does what sticky tape does. Fifteen years later the workshop is bigger and the opinion is unchanged.

Conservation is our default, not our upsell — acid-free boards, reversible hinging, UV glass — because the watercolour you bring in casually is usually the one that turns out to matter. The chest of mouldings runs from austere oak to a gilt so theatrical we keep it behind a curtain.

The advice is the free part and we take it seriously. Sometimes the right answer is the cheaper frame. Sometimes it's no frame at all and the name of a good conservator. You'll get the true answer either way.

House principles.

PRINCIPLE I

Conservation by default.

Acid-free board, reversible hinges, UV glass — on everything, unless you actively decline. The picture you don't think matters is the one that will.

PRINCIPLE II

Mitres cut by hand.

Machine-perfect corners exist; ours are better, because wood has grain and gilt has direction and a hand notices. Check our corners with a fingernail. People do.

PRINCIPLE III

Advice against our interest.

We regularly recommend the cheaper moulding, and occasionally no frame at all. A framer you trust for fifty years is worth more than one expensive sale.

PLATE VII / ASKED AT THE TABLEPIECE IN HAND

Asked at the table.

Anything else — ring 01284 705 331. Mid-glue we can talk; mid-gild we cannot, and you'd respect why.

How much does framing cost?

It scales with size, moulding and glazing, so quotes happen at the table with the piece in front of us — free, in twenty minutes, with three honest options. The cheapest one will be genuinely good; that's the house rule.

How long does it take?

Two to three weeks for most work; conservation and box frames a little longer. If there's a birthday or a court deadline (it happens), tell us — the workshop can occasionally perform miracles for a biscuit.

What is conservation framing and do I need it?

Acid-free materials, reversible mounting and UV glazing — framing that can be undone in fifty years without a trace. You need it for anything original, signed, sentimental or irreplaceable. For a poster you'll replace next year, we'll say so and save you money.

Can you fix acid burn / foxing / old tape?

We can stop it getting worse and re-house the piece safely, and for serious surgery we refer to a paper conservator we trust. What we won't do is "have a go" — a framer knowing that boundary is exactly what you're paying for.

Can you frame [extremely weird object]?

The current record holder is a taxidermied puffin, framed with dignity. Shirts, medals, ballet shoes, wedding bouquets, circuit boards — if it fits through the door and means something, we'll build a box around it.

Do you sell ready-made frames?

A small shelf of standard sizes in our own mouldings, for the certificates and photos of life. If your piece is any way off-standard, bespoke costs less than you fear and fits like it should.
