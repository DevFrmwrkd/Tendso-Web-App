The house

Quiet is the whole product.

Stille opened in 2020 in the shell of a 1920s tram depot in Amsterdam Oost. We kept the brick, the roof trusses and the echo, and poured two warm pools, a cold plunge, two saunas and a steam room into the middle of it. The name means 'quiet', and we enforce it kindly: no phones past the lockers, voices low, no clocks on the walls.

Sessions run two hours and are capped at twenty guests — roughly half the number the room could hold. Twice a day a sauna master runs an aufguss ritual that is worth planning around.

The massage and breathwork studios sit off the main hall, behind a heavy curtain. Practitioners are employed, not rented a room — the person who treats you in March is the person who treats you in November.

Held to, daily

Three quiet promises.

I

Capped at twenty, always.

The room could hold twice that. It never will. You will always find a place to lie down, swim, and sit in silence.

II

No phones, no clocks.

Both stay in the lockers. Two hours is long enough that you stop counting them — that is the point.

III

Practitioners on staff.

Massage and breathwork are given by people employed here, trained here, and still here next season.

A first visit

How a session goes.

  1. One

    Book a session

    Online, up to four weeks ahead. Morning silence and weekend evenings go first.

  2. Two

    Arrive fifteen early

    Check in, lockers, robe and towels. The phone goes in the small safe; you keep the key on your wrist.

  3. Three

    Take the circuit

    Warm, hot, cold, rest — in whatever order your body argues for. The card in the changing room helps first-timers.

  4. Four

    Leave slowly

    The rest hall has broth, tea and low chairs. Nobody will hurry you out; the next session simply flows in.

Asked at the desk

Before your first visit.

Anything else — ring +31 20 210 44 78 or write to the front desk. We answer between sessions.

Do I need to bring anything?

Nothing. Robe, towels, sandals, oils and hair ties are included. Swimwear is required in the pools and optional in the saunas — the two zones are signed.

Is it mixed?

Yes, all sessions are mixed. Tuesday evening is women-only; the last Sunday evening of the month is men-only. Both are marked in the booking calendar.

I've never done a cold plunge. Will anyone push me?

No one will push you into anything, ever. The card in the changing room suggests a gentle first circuit, and staff are happy to talk you through it.

Can I book massage without a bath session?

Yes, though most guests pair them. Massage-only bookings enter through the side door and still get the rest hall afterwards.

Is the bathhouse accessible?

Step-free throughout, a pool hoist, an accessible changing room, and one adapted massage table. Tell us when you book so we can hold the nearest locker.

Can I come pregnant?

The warm pools and rest hall, yes, throughout. Saunas and the cold plunge come with standard medical cautions — our pregnancy-trained masseuse can advise, and your midwife outranks us.

What if I fall asleep in the rest hall?

You will not be the first or the fifth that day. Staff wake rest-hall sleepers ten minutes before session end, gently.

Is there an age limit?

Eighteen and up. Quiet is fragile.
