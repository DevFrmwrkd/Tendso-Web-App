DE ZAAK

A workshop with a shop attached.

Spoke & Sprocket opened in 2011 as two repair stands and a coffee machine in a former butcher's on Kanaalstraat. The tiles are original. So is the coffee machine, regrettably.

The workshop is the heart: four stands, two mechanics, and a same-day promise on most jobs booked before ten. The shop grew around it — city bikes that survive Dutch weather, cargo bikes for families and firms, and a wall of kids' bikes that trade back in when the kids outgrow them.

We sell what we can fix and fix what we sell. If a bike is cheaper to repair than replace, we'll say so and lose the sale. That policy has cost us thousands of euros and made us most of our regulars.

DE WERKPLAATS

Shop & street.

VRAGEN

Asked at the bench.

Anything else — call +31 30 231 88 04 or roll by. Someone's always at the stands.

Do I need an appointment for a flat?

No. Roll in, we fix it while you wait — usually fifteen minutes, longer on Monday mornings when half of Lombok arrives at once.

How does same-day service work?

Book a bench slot online before ten and most jobs are done by six the same day. We text when it's ready. If a part has to be ordered, we tell you before we start.

Do you service e-bikes bought elsewhere?

Yes — Bosch, Shimano and Bafang systems, with certified diagnostics. Supermarket e-bikes we'll look at honestly, but some are built not to be fixable, and we'll say so.

What's the kids' bike trade-in worth?

Roughly a third of what you paid, as credit toward the next size — if it comes back in rideable shape. We refurbish it and it goes back on the wall.

Do you sell second-hand bikes?

Refurbished, yes — serviced top to bottom with a six-month workshop warranty. Stock changes weekly; the good ones go fast.

Can I test-ride a cargo bike properly?

Take one for a weekend. Groceries, kids, the works. A ten-minute parking-lot loop tells you nothing about a bakfiets.

Do you do fleet accounts?

Yes — delivery firms and offices get priority bench slots, monthly invoicing, and a loaner pool so nobody stands still.

My bike was stolen. Now what?

Report it, check the police depot, and come talk to us — we keep frame numbers of every bike we sell and we'll flag it to the shops on the street.
