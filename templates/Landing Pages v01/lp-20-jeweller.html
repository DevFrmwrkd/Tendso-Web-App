The atelier

One bench. No cases of stock.

Meridian & Grey is not a jewellery shop. There are no glass cases, no trays brought out in velvet, no 'collection'. There is a bench, a safe, two chairs for you, and a goldsmith who trained eight years in Pelikaanstraat workshops before opening her own door in 2013.

Everything here is made to order or remade from what you bring. Inherited pieces — the brooch nobody wears, the chain with a story but no style — are redesigned with their history kept on purpose.

Stones are sourced two streets away, with certificates. Gold is recycled where the design allows. Every piece leaves with its drawings, so any goldsmith on earth could repair it in fifty years.

The work

Six things made at this bench.

i.

Bespoke engagement rings

From first conversation to finished ring, six to ten weeks. Three design proposals, one honest recommendation.

ii.

Wedding bands

Made as a pair, at the bench, often with your involvement — one afternoon of filing yours is tradition here.

iii.

Redesign of inherited pieces

Old gold, old stones, new life. The history stays; the setting changes. We photograph everything before the first cut.

iv.

Repairs & restoration

Ring sizing, chain repair, stone tightening, re-tipping claws. Honest triage — some things shouldn't be touched.

v.

Stone sourcing

Diamonds and coloured stones from the quarter, certificated, at stated margins. You see exactly what we pay.

vi.

Valuations

Insurance valuations with photographs and full specifications, accepted by every Belgian insurer.

Held to

Three uncommon practices.

The maker is the seller.

The person who designs your ring makes your ring. Nothing is sent out, nothing is white-labelled.

Stated margins on stones.

You see the dealer's invoice. Our margin is fixed and printed on the quote. Nobody does this; we think everybody should.

Drawings included, forever.

Every piece leaves with its technical drawings. Any goldsmith, anywhere, in any decade, can maintain it.

The commission

From idea to hand.

  1. I

    Talk

    An hour at the atelier. Bring photos, heirlooms, half-formed ideas. Coffee is included; commitment is not.

  2. II

    Sketch & quote

    Three proposals with fixed prices inside two weeks. One will be our honest recommendation.

  3. III

    Make

    Six to ten weeks at the bench. You're welcome to visit mid-make; wax fittings for complex pieces.

  4. IV

    Collect

    Fitted, photographed, documented. The drawings go home with you, along with the care instructions.

Asked at the bench

Before your appointment.

What does a bespoke ring cost?

Design and making start from a stated bench fee, plus materials at documented cost and our fixed, printed margin. The three proposals we sketch always include one at the bottom of your stated budget — treated as seriously as the others.

How long does it take?

Engagement rings, six to ten weeks from approved sketch. Wedding bands, four to six. Repairs, usually under two weeks. If your proposal has a date, tell us — we've done heroics before and will again.

Can you work with my stones and gold?

Almost always. Old gold can be re-alloyed and reused if the design allows; stones are checked for chips and re-cut only with your consent. We photograph everything before the first cut.

Are your diamonds certificated?

Every diamond above 0.30ct comes with a GIA or HRD certificate, and you see the dealer invoice alongside our fixed margin. Lab-grown is available and clearly labelled — we sell both without sermons either way.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes — the atelier is a workshop, not a shop floor. Appointments are an hour, unhurried, and free. Evenings possible on Thursdays.

Do you do repairs on pieces made elsewhere?

Yes, with honest triage first. Some things are riskier to repair than to leave; we'll tell you which yours is and why, before anything is agreed.

What about valuations?

Insurance valuations with full specifications and photography, accepted by every Belgian insurer we've encountered. Turnaround one week.

Can we watch our rings being made?

Mid-make visits are welcome, and couples can spend a supervised afternoon filing their own wedding bands. It's the most-mentioned line in our guestbook.
