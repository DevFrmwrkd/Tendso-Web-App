MARK 01 / THE BENCHMAKER TAKES YOUR BRIEF

A ring is the smallest thing you'll wear the longest.

We opened Ashcroft & Vane in 2009 after years at other people's benches, tired of watching couples sold shiny things made in a factory and marked up four times over. We wanted a room where the person who takes your brief is the person who makes the ring — and where you can see the metal move under the hammer.

Everything is made by hand at the bench: drawn, forged, filed, set and finished here in London. We work in recycled and traceable gold and platinum, and we deal only with stones whose origins we can stand behind — you'll always know where a diamond or sapphire came from before it goes into your ring.

We also love old gold. Bring in a grandmother's ring you never wear and we'll remodel it into something you will — same metal, same memory, new life. A jeweller who only sells new has forgotten that the best gold usually already belongs to you.

MARK 07 / ASKED AT THE BENCHBEFORE YOU COMMIT

Asked at the bench.

How long does a bespoke ring take to make?

For an engagement or wedding ring made from scratch, allow roughly four to six weeks from the design being agreed — longer if we're sourcing a particular stone or working a complex setting. Remodelling and repairs are usually quicker. We'll always give you an honest timeline at the bench and, if there's a wedding date bearing down, we'll tell you straight whether we can hit it rather than promise and disappoint. Rush jobs are sometimes possible; we'd rather you asked than assumed.

Can you use my own gold or an old ring?

Yes, and we love it when you do. We can melt down and rework your own gold, or reset stones from a piece you no longer wear into something you will. It keeps the metal and the memory and often costs less than starting from raw materials. There are limits — very worn or low-carat gold sometimes isn't ideal to reuse structurally — and we'll tell you honestly if that's the case and suggest the best way to honour the original piece.

How do I know where your diamonds and stones come from?

We work only with stones whose supply chain we can trace, and we'll show you the paperwork before you commit. For diamonds that means conflict-free sourcing and, where you want it, lab-grown or certified natural stones with full documentation. For coloured stones we deal with cutters and dealers we know personally. Provenance matters to us as much as it does to you, and we'd never ask you to take a stone's origin on faith.

What metals do you work in?

Mostly 18ct and 9ct gold in yellow, white and rose, and platinum — all from recycled and traceable sources. We can advise on which suits your hand, your lifestyle and your budget: platinum for hard-wearing white, 18ct for richness and colour, 9ct where budget matters and durability is a plus. We'll also be honest about upkeep — white gold needs occasional re-plating, platinum patinas rather than wears away — so you choose knowing how the ring will age.

Do you do repairs and resizing on rings not made by you?

We do — resizing, claw re-tipping, re-shanking, soldering, stone tightening, pearl restringing and rescuing heirlooms other jewellers have given up on. All of it is done at our own bench in London, not parcelled off to a trade workshop you'll never meet. Bring the piece in and we'll assess it honestly, quote before we start, and tell you if a repair genuinely isn't worth doing rather than take your money for a lost cause.

Do I need an appointment?

For a proper consultation — designing a ring, remodelling gold, choosing a stone — yes, please book, so we can give you an unhurried hour at the bench with the right person and the right materials to hand. For repairs, resizing quotes and quick questions you're welcome to drop in during opening hours. Booking ahead simply means you won't find us mid-setting with our loupe down and no time to do you justice.
