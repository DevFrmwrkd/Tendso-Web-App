POUR 01 / THE BARTHE MENU IS THE BARTENDER

The menu is the bartender.

The Blind Tiger opened in 2016 with a printed menu like everyone else. We binned it within a month. A menu freezes a bar in time; it tells you what we made last season, not what's ripe, not what just came off the still, not what suits the weather and your mood tonight. So we threw it out and put the knowledge behind the bar instead.

You give three coordinates — a base spirit, a mood, a strength — and one of four bartenders builds from there, asking as much as they need to. It's not a magic trick; it's forty years of combined experience and a back bar of three hundred bottles doing what a laminated card can't.

Twelve seats, deliberately. You can see every drink made, ask what's in it, and change your mind halfway. Walk-ins before eight; reservations after. The room is small so the attention isn't.

POUR 03 / WHY NO MENUTHREE REASONS, NO CARD

Why we threw the menu out.

01 / 03

No menu, no ceiling.

A menu limits a bar to what fit on the card. We're limited only by three hundred bottles and what the bartender knows — which is a great deal more than a page holds.

02 / 03

Twelve seats, full attention.

Small on purpose. You watch your drink made, ask what's in it, and get a bartender who has time for the answer. Big rooms can't do this; we won't try.

03 / 03

Built for you, not for Instagram.

No smoke, no dry ice, no drink that photographs better than it tastes. Balance over theatre, every time. The garnish earns its place or it doesn't get one.

POUR 04 / THE ORDERSEAT → COORDINATES → DRINK
  1. Step 01

    Take a seat

    Twelve at the bar. Walk in before eight or reserve after. No standing three-deep waving a twenty — that's not what this is.

  2. Step 02

    Give three coordinates

    A spirit, a mood, a strength. "Gin, something bright, medium" is plenty. The more you tell us, the closer we land.

  3. Step 03

    Watch it built

    Everything happens in front of you. Ask what that bottle is, why that ice, what they're tasting for. Curiosity is the house drink.

  4. Step 04

    Adjust and go again

    Too sweet? Too strong? Say so — we recalibrate on the next one. By drink three we know you. That's the whole point of no menu.

POUR 07 / ASKED ACROSS THE BARUSUALLY MID-STIR

Asked across the bar.

Anything else — ring 0161 228 7412 or message us. After eight we're busy pouring; leave it and we'll come back to you.

Is there really no menu at all?

None. Not hidden, not on request, not a QR code. You tell the bartender a spirit, a mood and a strength, and they build from there. If you genuinely can't decide, "surprise me" is an accepted and encouraged order.

What if I don't know what I like?

Perfect — that's the easy version. Tell us a flavour you enjoy in anything (coffee, elderflower, chilli, black pepper) or a drink you had once and loved, and we'll triangulate. Half the fun is watching a vague answer become a specific drink.

Do you take reservations?

Walk-ins before 8pm, reservations after — twelve seats go fast, so booking the later slots is wise. Reserve online or ring. Groups larger than four are better as a private hire; the bar seats work best in ones, twos and threes.

How much is a drink?

Comparable to any serious cocktail bar in the city — you're paying for spirits and skill, not theatre. Because there's no menu, tell your bartender a ceiling if you have one and they'll build beautifully inside it. No one's ever surprised by the bill here.

Can you do non-alcoholic drinks?

Properly, yes — distilled zero-proof spirits, house shrubs, clarified juices, built with the same three-coordinate care as everything else. Drivers and non-drinkers get the full experience, not a consolation prize.

I have an allergy / I hate coriander / aniseed is my enemy.

Tell us — it's exactly the kind of thing the no-menu format handles best. We route around allergies and hatreds live, which a printed menu can't. The more we know, the better the drink; nothing you say is too fussy.
