A/C 01 — THE FIRMSECOND GENERATION

Small firm. On purpose. Since 1979.

Ledger & Sons is the family name, and yes, we've heard every joke. The firm was started by Ian Ledger in 1979 with a calculator, a filing cabinet, and the view that an accountant's job is to make the client's year quieter. Two of the current three partners are his sons; the third out-argues them both.

We act for around four hundred clients and know most of them by voice. The phone gets answered by someone who can actually answer the question.

Fees are fixed for the year, agreed in writing before any work starts, and never padded by surprise "phone call charges". If you ring with a quick question, the answer is included. That's the whole point of having an accountant.

A/C 07 — ENQUIRIESASKED ACROSS THE DESK

Asked across the desk.

Anything else — ring 01786 448 220. An accountant answers. That's not a slogan, it's the rota.

What does 'fixed fee' actually cover?

Everything in your engagement letter: the accounts, returns, filings, payroll if agreed — and the phone calls, emails and "quick questions" in between. Genuinely new work (an HMRC enquiry, a company purchase) is quoted separately before starting. You will never receive a bill you didn't see coming.

How painful is switching accountants?

For you: one email giving your old accountant permission to talk to us. For us: a standard professional handover we've done hundreds of times. Nobody's feelings get hurt; it's the most routine letter in accountancy.

I'm behind on my returns. How much trouble am I in?

Less than you fear, more than you'd like. Come in this week — penalties grow with delay, and HMRC treats voluntary disclosure far more kindly than discovery. We've unpicked worse than yours. Genuinely, whatever it is: worse.

Do you work with cloud bookkeeping software?

Yes — we set clients up on the mainstream platforms and train you on the fifteen minutes a week that keep things clean. We're deliberately not tied to one vendor.

Sole trader or limited company?

The most common first-meeting question, and the answer is an honest "it depends" with numbers attached. We'll model both against your actual situation and give you a recommendation, not a shrug.

What do you need from me and when?

Records after your year-end, ideally by autumn. A shoebox of receipts is accepted with a sigh and a small smile; a tidy folder is accepted with visible joy. Either way it gets done.

Are you taking new clients?

Yes, at a pace that keeps the phone answerable. If we're at capacity we'll say so and suggest a date rather than letting service quietly rot.
