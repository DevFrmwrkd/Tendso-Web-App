COUNT 01 / THE STUDIOMIRRORS CURTAINED, ON PURPOSE

A studio for the ones who almost did.

Everyone who walks in here has a version of the same story: danced as a kid and stopped, always wanted to and never started, or watched from the wings while life happened. Our founder — twelve years with a touring contemporary company, one retired knee — built this studio specifically for that person.

Classes are small and levelled honestly. Beginner means beginner: first positions, no assumed vocabulary, no teenager in the corner making it look effortless. Improvers move up when they're ready, which is a conversation, not an exam. There are no exams anywhere in the building.

The floor is sprung, the piano is real on Saturdays, and the mirrors have curtains — drawn for the first month of every beginners' course, on purpose. You learn to feel it before you're asked to watch it.

I have genuinely never danced. Not once.

Then you're our exact target audience. Ballet from zero and beginner contemporary assume nothing — not the vocabulary, not the flexibility, not the childhood lessons. Everyone in the room signed up for the same reason you did.

Am I too old for this?

The phrase "at my age" is banned in the building, and we mean it. Current class range: 19 to 74. Bodies vary; the teaching adjusts. The 74-year-old has better posture than the teachers.

What do I wear?

Anything you can move in. Ballet: socks or bare feet to start — buy shoes at week four if you're staying, and we'll tell you which ones. Tap shoes can be borrowed from the studio rack for your first term.

I'm coming back after twenty years. Which level?

Book a chat, not a class — fifteen minutes with a teacher, a bit of movement, an honest recommendation. Returners almost always underestimate themselves; the body files dance away safely and hands it back faster than you'd think.

Do you do performances?

One optional studio sharing a year, in-house, friendly audience, no sequins unless requested. About half the studio takes part. The other half applauds with drinks. Both are correct.

Is the floor really sprung?

Properly sprung — basket-weave batten construction under semi-sprung panels, the same build as professional studios. Your knees, shins and future self are the beneficiaries. It's also why we ask for clean indoor shoes.
