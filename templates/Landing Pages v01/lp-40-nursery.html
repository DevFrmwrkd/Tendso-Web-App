HORT. 01 / THE NURSERYTWO GROWERS, ONE OPINIONATED DOG

Grown here means grown HERE.

Most "local" nurseries buy in Dutch trolleys of forced stock and sell them within the month. Ours is the old kind of nursery: we propagate, pot on, and grow our plants in the beds you walk past on your way in. The stock list is shorter. The plants are harder. That's the trade and we made it deliberately.

Twenty-two years at this altitude has produced an honest catalogue: what you see thriving in our display garden — through frost pocket, clay, and the wind off the moor — is what we propagate. Plants that sulked here got discontinued, however fashionable.

There are two of us plus a dog with strong opinions about hostas. Ask us anything; refusing to sell you the wrong plant is the most useful service we offer.

HORT. 07 / ASKED AT THE BENCHESTROWEL IN HAND

Asked at the benches.

Anything else — ring 01434 601 552. If we're at the far end of the field, leave a message with the dog. Or the machine.

Why is your range smaller than a garden centre's?

Because we only sell what we grow, and we only grow what survives here. A garden centre's range is a wholesaler's range. Ours is a survivors' list — shorter, but everything on it means it.

Will your plants survive my garden?

If your garden is in the North East, very probably — our field is colder, windier and wetter than almost anywhere you'd plant. Tell us your specifics and we'll flag the exceptions honestly.

When should I plant?

Perennials: spring or autumn, autumn quietly better. Bare-root anything: November to March. Container stock: whenever you can get a spade in. The honest answer is "sooner than you think and deeper than you did".

Do you deliver?

Within about twenty miles, yes — Tynedale, Corbridge, along the wall. Trees and bulk orders we'll always deliver and usually help unload. Bring a wheelbarrow to the van, we'll do the rest.

Can you help me plan a border?

Yes — bring photos, rough measurements and the jam jar of soil. Planting plans are priced by the job and credited back against the plants. We plan with our list, which is the point of asking us.

What's your guarantee?

Hardy stock that fails in its first year comes back for replacement, no theatre — bring the corpse and the label. What we can't guarantee: drainage you haven't fixed and rabbits you haven't met yet.
