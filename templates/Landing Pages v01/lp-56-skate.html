SESSION 01 / THE SHOPFROG ISLAND, LEICESTER

Built by skaters. Obviously.

Bearings started as a shop with a mini-ramp in the back that got out of hand. Ten years on it's a full 6,000 sq ft indoor park with a shop attached, run by people who close early sometimes because the conditions outside are perfect and it would be rude not to.

We build decks on the counter — grip cut, trucks set, bearings pressed, wheels on — while you wait, sized to how you actually skate, not what's on the poster. Ask a daft question; there are no daft questions here, only the ones nobody answered when we started.

The park runs sessions for every level, from never-stood-on-one to sponsored. Helmets on for under-18s, no exceptions, and a coaching crew who remember exactly how terrifying the first drop-in feels because they filmed each other doing it badly.

SESSION 03 / THE DOOR POLICYSPRAYED ON THE WALL

Why here, not there.

1

Skater-owned, skater-run.

Everyone behind the counter rides. Your setup advice comes from people who'll be in the park an hour after their shift, not from a training manual.

2

No attitude at the door.

Skate shops have a reputation and we've spent ten years being the opposite of it. Beginners, kids, returners, and the nervous are the whole point, not the tolerated.

3

Open whatever the weather.

Indoor, floodlit, heated. The British summer is a rumour and the British winter is a fact; the park doesn't care about either. Ride year-round.

SESSION 07 / ASKED AT THE COUNTERUSUALLY BEFORE THE FIRST DROP-IN

Asked at the counter.

Anything else — ring 0116 442 7715. If the park's loud, message us; someone's always near a phone.

I've genuinely never skated. Can I come?

That's who the quiet-hours and beginner sessions are built for. Boards and pads to borrow, coaches who float the whole time, and a room full of people who were also terrible once. Everyone starts on the flat; nobody starts in the bowl.

Is it safe for kids?

Helmets mandatory for under-18s, pads strongly pushed, coached sessions with proper ratios, and a first-aider on site every session. Skating has risk — we manage it hard and teach falling as a skill, because it is one.

Do I need my own board?

No — borrow ours to start, and buy when you're hooked (you will be). When you do, we build it on the counter sized to you, which beats anything off a website. Bring your own anytime; we'll tune it up.

How much is a session?

Priced per session with cheaper multi-passes and a membership if you're in three-plus times a week. Kids' and quiet-hours sessions are their own rate. Current prices are on the board and the Google profile; they don't hide.

Can adults skate here or is it a kids' thing?

Both, deliberately. Adult beginners are one of our biggest groups — there are dedicated adult sessions where the average age surprises everyone. It is never too late; our oldest regular is 63 and better than most.

Can I hire the park?

Yes — birthdays, groups, stag and hen dos, work team-builds. Whole-park or a coached zone, boards and pads included, a coach or two on the floor. Ring ahead; weekends book up fast.
