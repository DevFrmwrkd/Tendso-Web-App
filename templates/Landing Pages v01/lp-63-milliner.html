BLOCK 01 / THE WORKROOMSTEAM · STRAW · WOODEN BLOCK

Steam, straw, and a wooden block.

A proper hat is built, not bought. At Crown & Brim the felt or straw is softened with steam, pulled over a wooden block carved for the shape, pinned, dried, wired and trimmed by hand over days. It's slow, it's a little bit alchemy, and it's why our hats sit where they're meant to and stay there when the wind gets ideas.

The studio started for Cheltenham race week — a town that takes its hats seriously has to — and grew into weddings, mother-of-the-bride headpieces, and a devoted following who simply like wearing a good hat to the shops. We block for all of it, and we hire for the once-in-a-lifetime days you'd rather not buy for.

Everything is fitted. We measure your head properly, ask about your outfit and your hair, and — crucially — read how much hat you actually want to wear. The best hat is the boldest one you'll still feel like yourself in. Finding that line is the whole job.

How far ahead should I book for the races or a wedding?

For bespoke, six to eight weeks is comfortable — blocking, drying and trimming can't be rushed and a mid-way fitting matters. Closer than that, talk to us anyway; the hire rail and semi-bespoke reblocks can rescue a shorter timeline more often than you'd think.

I've never worn a hat and I'm nervous. Help.

That's most first-timers, and it's the best possible starting point. We fit against your face, your hair and your nerve, and land on a hat you feel like yourself in. Nobody leaves in something that wears them; that's a promise, not a slogan.

Bespoke or hire — which is right for me?

Hire is ideal for a one-off day you won't repeat — a single wedding, a big race. Bespoke is for a hat you'll wear for years, or a bridal piece that has to be exactly right. We'll tell you honestly which suits your occasion and budget; sometimes the answer is hire, and we say so.

Can you match a specific outfit colour?

Yes — bring a swatch, the actual garment, or a very good photo. We dye and trim to match, and we're candid when an exact match would look worse than a considered contrast. Matching the hat to the shoes is usually a happier plan than matching it to the dress.

Do you reblock or restore old hats?

Gladly — it's some of our favourite work. A squashed felt, an inherited hat that needs resizing, a tired trim: most can be steamed, reblocked and re-trimmed back to life. Bring it in and we'll assess honestly whether it's worth the work.

How do I keep a hat on in the wind?

A properly fitted hat and the right hatpins do most of it — the fit is why bespoke stays put where elastic doesn't. At the final fitting we show you exactly how to pin it to your hair, and send you off with pins and the confidence to trust them.
