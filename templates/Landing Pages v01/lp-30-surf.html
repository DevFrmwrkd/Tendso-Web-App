01 / THE SHOP50M ABOVE THE TIDELINE

Fifty metres from the tideline.

Groundswell started as two coaches, a van, and a rail of wetsuits that smelled exactly how you'd imagine. Fifteen years later there's a proper shop, a drying room that has changed lives, and a coaching team that has taught upwards of twenty thousand first waves.

The teaching philosophy is simple: the ocean sets the lesson plan. Coaches read the day's conditions and adapt — and some days the sea says no, and we say it honestly and rebook you free.

The daily report is written at 7am by whoever surfed first. It will tell you the truth, including "don't bother today".

07 / FAQASKED AT THE COUNTER, DRIPPING

Before you paddle out.

Anything else — ring 01637 852 774. If the surf's good, leave a message. Priorities.

I've never surfed and I'm not a strong swimmer.

Beginners' lessons happen in waist-deep water and you keep the board — it's a giant flotation device. You need to be comfortable in the sea, not fast in a pool. Tell us when you book and the coach will keep you shoreward of the group.

What's included in a lesson?

Board, suit (plus boots and hood in winter), beach briefing, two hours of coaching in the water, and the hot shower afterwards, which in February is the best part.

What if the sea is flat or dangerous?

We tell you the night before and rebook free, or refund if the trip doesn't allow it. We cancel for danger and for flatness — a lesson in nothing teaches nothing.

How cold is it really?

Cornish water runs about 8°C in March and 17°C in September. In a proper 5/4 with boots you'll be genuinely fine — cold hands after 90 minutes in deep winter, honestly disclosed.

Am I too old to learn?

Our oldest first-timer this decade was 71. If you can get from lying down to standing on land — however inelegantly — the sea will meet you halfway.

Do you run private lessons?

One-to-one and family privates, any day the sea cooperates. Same price logic: one coach, your pace, video included.

Can I just hire a board?

If you can demonstrate basic competence (we'll ask three questions and watch you carry it), yes. If you're new, one lesson first — the sea here has rips that don't negotiate.
