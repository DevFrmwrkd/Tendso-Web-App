01 / STUDIOHALDEN & RO

A small studio, deliberately.

Halden & Ro was founded in 2015 by two architects who met restoring a 1930s functionalist villa in Klampenborg and decided they never wanted to work at scale again. The studio has grown to nine — four architects, two constructing architects, an interior architect, a model-maker, and a project lead who used to be a carpenter.

We take eleven projects a year. That number is not a slogan; it is how many a studio of nine can carry while drawing every junction, visiting every site weekly, and answering the phone when the contractor calls at 7am.

Most of our work is residential. We also take one small public or cultural project a year, at cost, chosen by the whole studio.

04 / PROCESSSKETCH → SITE

How a project unfolds.

  1. PHASE 01

    Conversation

    An hour at the studio or on your site. No fee, no deck, no hard sell. We both decide if it fits.

  2. PHASE 02

    Sketch phase

    Three to six weeks. One strong proposal and the reasoning behind it — not five weak options.

  3. PHASE 03

    Drawing & tender

    Full construction drawings, tendered to two or three contractors we trust with the work.

  4. PHASE 04

    Site

    Weekly visits, a shared decision log, and a handover walk with a snag list we actually chase.

07 / QUESTIONSASKED OFTEN

Before you write to us.

If yours isn't answered here, it belongs in the first conversation — studio@haldenro.dk.

What budgets do you work with?

Renovations typically start around 2.5m DKK of construction cost; new-builds higher. If your budget is below that we will say so in the first hour and point you to smaller practices we rate.

How long does a project take?

A full apartment renovation runs 12–18 months from first sketch to handover; a new-build 18–30. Roughly half of that is drawing and permissions, half is site.

Do you work outside Copenhagen?

Within about ninety minutes of the studio — North Zealand, Møn, and occasionally further for the right site. Weekly site presence is non-negotiable, so distance has a real limit.

Can we hire you for just a sketch phase?

Yes. About a third of clients start with a sketch phase alone and decide afterwards. The sketch fee is fixed and quoted upfront.

How are your fees structured?

Fixed fee per phase, quoted before the phase begins. No percentages, no hourly surprises. Changes of scope are re-quoted, in writing, before we draw a line.

Do you do interiors without building work?

Yes — full interior architecture is a standalone service, from joinery drawings to maker tendering.

Will you work with our contractor?

If they are good, gladly. We will meet them before committing — the project is only as calm as its site.
