01 / THE STUDIONORTH LIGHT, ALL DAY

North light, no nonsense.

North of Ninety is on the third floor of a Victorian warehouse in Merchant City, chosen for one reason: six north-facing windows, each nearly three metres tall. North light doesn't move all day. It's why painters fought over these buildings a century ago and why we fought a yoga studio for the lease in 2014.

Two photographers work here — one from newspapers, one from fashion — and a retoucher who used to restore paintings and treats skin the same way: repair, don't repaint.

When we're not shooting, the studio is yours to hire, lights included.

07 / FAQASKED BEFORE EVERY SHOOT

Before you're in front of it.

Anything else — ring 0141 552 8907. If we're shooting, the retoucher picks up.

What should I wear for a headshot?

Plain, mid-tone, well-fitting, and something you already feel good in. Bring two options; we'll tell you in ten seconds which one the light likes. Avoid fine stripes — sensors hate them.

How long until I get my photos?

Web gallery inside the week, retouched selects within 48 hours of you choosing. Press deadlines can be same-day — say so when you book.

How many photos do I get?

Portraits: every usable frame in the gallery, with retouching included on your selects (three for a standard session). More selects can be added per image.

I'm genuinely awkward in front of a camera. Will this be awful?

You're describing 90% of our clients, including the actors. The session is directed from first frame to last — you'll never be left standing wondering what to do.

Can I hire the studio without a photographer?

Yes — half-day and full-day hire includes lights, stands, and backdrops. First-time hirers get a fifteen-minute walkthrough of the kit and the fuse board.

Do you shoot on location?

Branding and editorial, yes — offices, workshops, wherever the story is. Portraits we keep in the studio, because the windows are the product.

Is the studio accessible?

There's a lift (passenger, not just freight) and a step-free route from the street. The studio WC is on the same floor.

Do you keep our photos private?

Nothing goes in our portfolio or feed without written permission, ever. Corporate libraries are stored encrypted and deleted on your schedule.
