SHOW 01 / THE WORKDESIGNED BY HAND, FIRED BY PROS

A firework is a full second of engineering, timed to the half-beat of your night.

Powder & Spark is a professional fireworks company based in Cheshire, firing displays across the North West and beyond. We started in 2005 doing village bonfire nights and grew into a team that now lights up weddings, festivals, corporate launches and civic celebrations — but the thrill has never faded, because there is nothing like the collective gasp of a crowd when the first shell breaks over their heads. We chase that gasp for a living.

Every display we fire is designed by hand for the specific occasion, site and budget, not pulled off a shelf. We choose each effect, colour and rhythm to build a story in the sky — a slow, elegant opening; a heart-stopping middle; a finale that empties the magazine — and we can choreograph the whole thing tightly to a piece of music that means something to you. Safety underpins all of it: BPA-trained firers, full public liability insurance, detailed risk assessments, and honest advice about what a site can and can't take.

We fire the full range — grand wedding displays, spectacular event shows, quieter low-noise displays that are kind to animals and young children, daytime and smoke effects, and pyromusical shows synced to the beat. We handle the licensing, the liaison with your venue, the setup and the safe firing, and we clear the site afterwards so all you're left with is the memory. Tell us the moment; we'll design the sky above it.

Before you book.

Anything else — ring 01625 555 340. Tell us the date and the moment, and we'll tell you honestly what's possible.

How far ahead should we book, and how does planning work?

Book as far ahead as you can, especially for popular wedding dates and the weeks around New Year and bonfire season, when we fill up months and sometimes a year in advance — but do ask even at short notice, because we can occasionally fit in a display with only a few weeks' warning. Planning starts with a conversation about your occasion, your venue and your budget, from which we design a display and give you a clear, itemised quote. Once you're happy and booked, we take over the technical side: liaising with your venue about the firing site and any restrictions, carrying out the risk assessment, arranging the licensing and notifying the relevant authorities where required, and confirming the running order and timings with you. In the final week we reconfirm everything and check the forecast. On the day we arrive early, set up, and fire to the agreed moment. Your job is simply to enjoy it; ours is everything else.

Are fireworks displays safe, and are you insured?

Safety is the foundation of everything we do, and a professional display fired by trained, insured operators is a world away from a back-garden box of fireworks. Our firers are BPA-trained (British Pyrotechnists Association), we carry full public liability insurance, and every single display is preceded by a detailed written risk assessment covering the firing site, the fallout zone, spectator distances, wind limits and emergency procedures. We inspect and confirm the site in advance, set safe distances for the crowd, monitor wind conditions on the night and will delay or, in genuinely unsafe conditions, stand down a display rather than fire it unsafely — that judgement is ours and it is non-negotiable. We fire professional-category fireworks that the public can't buy, from properly-engineered mortars and racks, under controlled conditions. In twenty years we've fired thousands of displays safely, and that record exists precisely because we treat safety as the whole trade, not an afterthought.

Can you choreograph the fireworks to music?

Yes — pyromusical displays, fired frame-tight to a piece of music, are the most spectacular thing we do and among our favourite commissions. Choosing the right track is part of the magic (a first-dance song, an anthem, a piece with real dynamic light and shade), and from it we compose the display so that every break, colour change and rhythmic run is timed to the beat, the crescendos crowned with the biggest shells and the quiet passages painted with elegant, slower effects. It's considerably more technical and more time-consuming to design and fire than a standard display, so it costs more and needs a little more planning and the right site, but the effect on an audience is simply on another level — people don't just watch it, they feel it. If you have a piece of music that matters to you and a moment worth marking, tell us; turning a song into ninety seconds of sky is exactly what we love to do.

What is a low-noise display, and is it as good?

A low-noise (or "quiet") display uses fireworks chosen for their colour, movement and beauty rather than their bang, giving you a genuinely spectacular show with only a fraction of the noise — and yes, it really is as beautiful, just quieter. It's an increasingly popular choice, and for good reason: it's far kinder to horses, dogs and other animals, to young children and noise-sensitive guests, and to neighbours, which makes it ideal for weddings at rural venues near livestock, for events in residential areas, or simply for anyone who loves the colour of fireworks but not the concussion. We design low-noise displays with the same care as any other, building colour, height and rhythm into a proper crescendo — you lose very little of the spectacle and gain a lot of goodwill. If your venue has concerns about noise, or you simply prefer a gentler show, ask us; we'll design something that still makes the crowd gasp, just without making the horses bolt.

What happens if the weather is bad on the night?

Fireworks and weather are old adversaries, and we plan for it so you don't have to worry. Light rain is generally fine — fireworks are surprisingly weatherproof and we protect the equipment — and a display will go ahead in most conditions. The real limiting factor is wind: strong or gusty wind, particularly blowing towards the crowd, affects the safe fallout zone, and if conditions on the night are genuinely unsafe we will delay the display to see if the wind drops and, as a last resort, stand it down for safety — a decision that is always ours to make and always errs on the side of caution. Because most of our work is for fixed occasions like weddings, an outright cancellation is rare and we'll always do everything possible to fire, including adjusting timing or firing position; where a display genuinely cannot go ahead for safety, we'll discuss rescheduling. We monitor the forecast closely in the run-up and keep you informed. Twenty years of reading British skies means we very rarely get caught out.

Do you only cover Cheshire, and how big can a display be?

We're based in Cheshire and much of our work is across the North West — Manchester, Liverpool, Chester and the surrounding counties — but we travel further afield for the right commission, so do ask wherever you are and we'll tell you honestly whether we're the right team for the location. As for scale, we fire everything from intimate close-proximity displays for small weddings, where a beautiful two-minute show fires from just beyond the guests, right up to large-scale event and civic displays for crowds of thousands with big shells and long, building finales — and, crucially, we scale the design and the budget to your occasion rather than pushing you towards more than you need. A small, well-designed display at the right moment can be every bit as moving as a huge one; a huge one, properly engineered, can take a crowd's breath clean away. Tell us your site, your guest numbers and your budget, and we'll design the biggest, best display that fits all three safely.
