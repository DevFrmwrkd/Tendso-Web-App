01 / THE DEPOTBUILT 1904 · REBOLTED WEEKLY

A tram depot with 200 problems.

Crimp City is what happened when three route-setters got the keys to a 1904 tram depot and a very patient bank manager. Eleven-metre ceilings, north skylights, and enough floor for four distinct bouldering zones plus a training area we're slightly too proud of.

The setting team is the product. Six setters, wall-by-wall resets every week, and a grading system that's argued about in the café like it's league football.

You don't need experience, a partner, or your own shoes. You need socks. Everything else is at the desk, including the humans who'll show you how to fall before you climb.

07 / FAQASKED AT THE DESK DAILY

Before you chalk up.

Anything else — ring 0117 954 3320. The desk answers between briefings.

I've never climbed. Can I just turn up?

Yes. Register online or at the desk, take the falling briefing, and you're in. If you'd rather have a human guide the first hour, book the First Timers session — it's the best money you'll spend here.

Do I need a partner?

No — that's the point of bouldering. The walls top out at 4.5m over thick matting. Half our regulars climb alone and leave with more friends than they came with.

What should I wear?

Anything you can move in. Socks are compulsory for rental shoes. Leave the rings and watches in the lockers — crimps eat jewellery.

How do the grades work?

Circuits by colour, green (easiest) to black (film crew territory). Tags carry a V-grade range. Everyone argues the grades; that's a feature, not a bug.

Is there an age limit?

Supervised kids from 5, youth club from 7, unsupervised climbing from 14 with a competency sign-off. The mezzanine is free for spectating parents — the coffee is not.

When is it quiet?

Weekday mornings and Friday nights (everyone's at the pub, mistakenly). Peak is 6–9pm Tuesday to Thursday. Live occupancy is on our Google profile.

Do you do parties and groups?

Birthday parties, stag/hen dos brave enough, corporate groups. Coached, catered by the café, booked at least two weeks out.
