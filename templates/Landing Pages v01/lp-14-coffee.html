THE ROASTERY

A roastery first. The bar is the tasting room.

Standard Deviation started in a garage in Coburg with a 1-kilo roaster and a spreadsheet nobody else could read. We moved to the Brunswick warehouse in 2017, upgraded to a 12-kilo Probat, and kept the spreadsheet.

The name is a promise about consistency: every batch is logged, curves are compared, and anything outside spec gets cupped, discussed, and — if it lost the argument — donated to the office-coffee bin.

The bar at the front exists so you can taste what we mean. Two espressos and two filters on at all times, rotated weekly.

FAQ

Asked over the tamper.

Anything else — ring (03) 9042 7714 or ask whoever's on the machine.

When is coffee freshest?

For espresso, 7–21 days off roast; for filter, 4–14. Every bag has the roast date on the front, not hidden in a fold. We won't sell you a bag older than three weeks.

Do you ship?

Australia-wide, free over $40. Roasted Tuesday or Thursday, shipped same day. Melbourne metro usually lands next day.

How does the subscription work?

Pick a frequency and a style (espresso, filter, or roaster's choice). Pause, skip or cancel from one link in every email. No minimum term.

What does wholesale cost?

The rate card is one page and we send it to anyone who asks. Pricing steps with volume; training and first-week dial-in are included at every tier.

Can I bring my own container?

Yes — we tare anything. There's also a return-and-refill scheme on 1kg tins for locals.

Is the Saturday cupping really free?

Really free, every Saturday at 10, no booking. Show up, slurp loudly, tell us what you taste. Kids welcome; dogs outside table only.

Do you have decaf that doesn't apologise?

A sugarcane-process Colombian, roasted weekly, cupped against the caffeinated table like everything else. We'd serve it to the roaster's mother.

Do you fix espresso machines?

By appointment — our tech does bench repairs Wednesday and Friday. Wholesale partners get priority and loaner machines.
