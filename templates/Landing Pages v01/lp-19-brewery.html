The brewery

Two arches, ten barrels, one obsession.

Gantry started in 2018 when two homebrewers took a lease on a leaking railway arch in Kirkstall and spent a winter learning why commercial breweries have drainage. The second arch came in 2021; the taproom bar is built from the scaffolding boards that held the first roof up.

We brew what we like drinking: proper pales, cask bitter with a straight face, dark beers for the six cold months, and one lager that took us three years to get right and we will not be taking questions about the previous attempts.

Every beer is brewed, fermented, canned and poured on this postcode. When a train crosses overhead the glasses ring on the shelf, and regulars raise theirs back.

Questions

Asked over the bar.

Anything else — ring 0113 887 2201 during taproom hours, or message any time.

Can I bring food in?

Yes — bring anything. The pizza van is outside Friday and Saturday, the bakery over the road does butties until three, and we have menus for every takeaway that delivers to an arch.

Are dogs really welcome?

There is a wall of polaroids of taproom dogs and a treat jar with a better budget than our marketing. Keep them leaded when it's busy.

Can I bring kids?

Until 8pm, yes — there are board games, crayons, and a strict no-darts-for-under-tens policy written in crayon by the last offender.

How do I book the tour?

Online, Saturdays at 2pm, eight people max. It's a working brewery so boots or trainers, not sandals. Four thirds of beer are included and the measures are honest.

Do you fill any growler?

Any clean vessel with a sensible neck. Ours are two pints; refills from any tap at a quid off.

Is the taproom accessible?

Step-free from the yard, an accessible loo in arch two, and a lowered bar section. The cobbles outside are Victorian and behave like it — call ahead and we'll meet you at the gate.

Do you serve anything besides beer?

A short natural wine list, a local cider, decent coffee, and three alcohol-free beers we actually rate — including our own pale at 0.5%.

Can pubs stock your beer?

Yes — cask and keg across Yorkshire with a fresh-rotation guarantee. Email trade@ and the brewer himself will probably answer.
