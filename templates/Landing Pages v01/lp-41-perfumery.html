FORMULA 01 / THE STUDIOSHOP IN FRONT · ORGAN IN BACK

A shop in front, an organ in back.

The back room holds the perfumer's organ — three hundred and forty materials racked in tiers, from bergamot you'd recognise to a smoked-birch note that clears the room in the best way. Every Bruma fragrance is composed and blended there, matured for six weeks, and bottled by hand in batches of sixty.

The house line stays at nine. When a tenth formula earns its place, a current one retires — buyers of retired numbers are warned in writing, given first refusal on the final batch, and consoled in person.

Fragrances are numbered because names lie. № 4 is not called "Midnight Whisper"; it is iris, carrot seed and cold air, and the card on the shelf says exactly that. You wear it for twenty minutes before we'll even discuss the bottle.

FORMULA 03 / METHODTHE THREE-LINE MANIFESTO

How we sell perfume.

LINE 1/3

Skin first, always.

Paper strips audition a perfume; skin decides. Twenty minutes minimum on your wrist before we'll discuss a bottle. It changes the answer more often than not.

LINE 2/3

The accord is on the card.

Every fragrance lists its actual materials. If you're allergic, curious or just suspicious of marketing, the truth is on the shelf in 10pt type.

LINE 3/3

Blended here, batch by batch.

Sixty bottles at a time, matured six weeks, batch number on the base. When you reorder, you're getting the same formula from the same book.

FORMULA 04 / A CONSULTATIONSTRIPS → SKIN → SLOWLY
  1. Step 01

    Talk first

    What you wear, what you hated, what the last bottle promised and didn't do. Honesty helps; grudges welcome.

  2. Step 02

    Six strips

    A structured shortlist from the nine — plus a wildcard we think you're wrong about. People are wrong about the wildcard 40% of the time.

  3. Step 03

    Two on skin

    Twenty minutes, a walk around the block, a coffee next door. Perfume is a time-based art; we give it time.

  4. Step 04

    Decide slowly

    Buy the bottle, take a sample, or leave with nothing but opinions. All three are good outcomes; two of them come back eventually.

FORMULA 07 / ASKED AT THE TABLEUSUALLY MID-SNIFF

Asked at the table.

Anything else — ring 01423 705 118 or message. Mid-consultation the phone waits; the nose in the chair has priority.

I don't know anything about perfume. Is that a problem?

It's the ideal starting condition. You know what you like the smell of — rain, tomato leaves, your grandfather's coat — and that's the entire qualification. The vocabulary is our job.

Why numbers instead of names?

Because "Velvet Dusk" tells you nothing and sets up a mood you'll try to smell. № 6 tells you nothing either — but the card underneath tells you everything: the accord, the materials, the batch. We'd rather you trust the card than the poetry.

How does bespoke work?

Three sittings over six to ten weeks. First: the brief — memories, materials, what it's for. Second: three trial accords on skin. Third: refinement and the final formula, logged under your name. It's remade on request, forever.

Do you sell samples?

2ml vials of the whole line, singly or as the full nine. It's the right way to buy perfume — a scent that dazzles in the shop and bores you by Thursday is a fact worth £6 to discover.

What about allergies and sensitivities?

Every formula's allergen list is printed and available before anything touches skin. Tell us what you react to; the organ is big enough to route around almost anything.

Can I bring my partner / mother / sceptical friend?

Please do — a second nose that knows you is genuinely useful data. Consultations seat two comfortably. The sceptical friends are our best converts; they fight hardest and buy fastest.
